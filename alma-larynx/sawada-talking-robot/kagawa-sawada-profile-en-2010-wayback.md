Profile of H.SAWADA 

## 
Hideyuki Sawada,

 Professor of 

Faculty of Engineering , 
Kagawa University 

 Profile: 

 August 7th, 1967: Born in
 Hanyu-city ,
 Saitama 

 March 1986: Graduated from
 Fudooka Highschool 

 March 1990: Graduated from

Waseda University 

 March 1992: Graduated from Waseda Graduate School (M.Eng.)

 April 1992 - March 1995: Worked in Mitsui Mining & Smelting Co. Ltd.

 April 1995 - March 1998: Research Fellow of the
 Japan Society for the
 Promotion of Science 

 February 1999: Received Dr.Eng. degree from Waseda University (Ph.D.)

 March 1999: Research Associate of Waseda University

 April 1999 - March 2010: Associate Professor of Kagawa University

 April 2010 - Present: Professor of Kagawa University
