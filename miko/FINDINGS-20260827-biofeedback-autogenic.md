## 2. The four lenses

### Lens A — autogenic training itself: **holds up**
Stetter & Kupper (2002), *Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback* — 73 controlled
studies 1952–99, **60 included, 35 of them RCTs**. Medium effect sizes against real
control conditions. Positive medium-range effects for tension headache/migraine,
mild-to-moderate hypertension, coronary heart disease, asthma, somatoform pain,
**Raynaud's disease**, anxiety, mild depression, functional sleep disorder.
Against *other* psychological treatments: no effect or small negative.
**Verdict: a real method with a real evidence base — and Raynaud's is precisely the
hand-warming/vasoconstriction channel.**

### Lens B — does adding the sensor beat the script? **No, in the trial that asked**
Freedman, Ianni & Wenig — 21 patients, three arms: autogenic training ·
progressive muscle relaxation · autogenic **plus skin-temperature feedback**.
Result, in their words: *"No significant differences between the three behavioral
treatment procedures were obtained."* All three improved digital temperature under
cold stress; none beat the others.
**Verdict: n=21 is small, but this is the direct test of my thesis and my thesis lost.**

### Lens C — the acquisition wall: **the real finding**
The **Raynaud's Treatment Study** — multicentre RCT, **N = 313**, temperature
biofeedback vs frontalis-EMG biofeedback vs nifedipine-XL vs placebo.

| arm | learned the target physiological response |
|---|---|
| temperature biofeedback | **34.6%** (81) |
| EMG biofeedback (behavioural control) | **55.4%** (74) |
| "normal" temperature biofeedback group | 67.4% (46) |

**Roughly two thirds of the people trained on hand-warming never acquired it — and the
biofeedback arm acquired its own skill LESS often than the control arm did.**
Predictors of success were coping style, anxiety, gender and clinic site — **not**
disease severity. **Verdict: skill acquisition, not hardware and not measurement, is
what the field is stuck on.**

### Lens D — light / AVE: **measurable, unproven, and the only dangerous part**
2025 review of audio-visual entrainment neuromodulation (PMC12564294). Protocols span
0.5–40 Hz; commonly 10, 14, 20, 40 Hz. Entrainment IS measurable on EEG during
stimulation — phase synchronisation rises in central and posterior cortex. But the
review states findings are *"heterogeneous and include null or limited effects"*, that
it remains unknown how long effects persist, and concludes that rigorous controlled
studies are needed before AVE can be called a reliable therapeutic tool.

**⚠️ SAFETY — the one item here that can injure a person.** That review reports
**~5% of people with epilepsy are susceptible to photic-induced seizures**, with higher
incidence among younger people and women, and that repetitive light is a documented
**migraine trigger**. Any light we ever build must be *structurally incapable* of the
dangerous band — not warned about, incapable. This is why VR is the right carrier.

Iddo said *"תבדוק באמצעים משפטיים... הכל חוקי וכשר"* and pointed at Internet Archive.
Looking for legal sources turned up the one body of work that breaks section 3 — and it
is **public domain**, because it is US government work.

### NASA Ames · Autogenic-Feedback Training Exercise (AFTE), Patricia S. Cowings

| | acquisition rate |
|---|---|
| Raynaud's Treatment Study — **single-channel** hand-temperature biofeedback | **34.6%** |
| **NASA AFTE — multi-channel, individualised** | **over 85%**, 200+ subjects |

NASA's own reports state the combined method is *"appreciably more effective than
either of the individual techniques used separately"* — the exact claim section 2 · Lens B
recorded as refuted.

**Why the two disagree, and it is not a contradiction.** Read the protocol: in an AFTE
session subjects are trained on **a pattern of up to 20 physiological responses at once**,
with many feedback channels, because — in NASA's own framing — the responses that matter
are **different for different people**. The clinical trials that failed were training
*one* channel and discarding everyone whose body does not speak through that channel.

**So my Lens B/C verdict was right about the design that was tested and wrong about the
design Iddo proposed.** He asked for *"בנץ' של כל מיני סנסורים"* — a bench of many sensors.
That is not a nice-to-have on top of the temperature idea. **It is the entire difference
between 34.6% and 85%.** He arrived at NASA's answer before I found NASA's papers, and
I argued against it from evidence about a narrower machine. Second time today I measured
the wrong thing (see the nasal-`ba` inquiry) — recorded, not softened.
