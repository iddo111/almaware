# מיקו · MIKO ruling — מחזורים בטבע ובכלכלה / CYCLES IN NATURE AND IN ECONOMICS

**Question (Iddo, 2026-08-28):** *"תביא כל המחקרים על מחזורים בטבע ובכלכלה"* — bring all the
research on cycles in nature and in economics.
**Four lenses: measured cycles in nature · the named economic cycles, graded · the statistics of
finding a cycle that is not there · where a cycle genuinely reaches a market.**
**לזכרה של עלמה ז״ל.**

**Evidence grades used throughout:**
**[M] MEASURED** — a mechanism is identified, the period is estimated from data, and independent
groups reproduce it · **[C] CONTESTED** — the number exists and competent people disagree about
what it means · **[B] BELIEVED** — asserted, and **not statistically distinguishable from noise**
by the tests in Lens C · **[F] FALSIFIED** — tested against a proper null and failed.

**Boundary, stated once:** this is a ruling about **statistics and mechanism**. It is not
investment advice and I am not a licensed advisor. Section 8 is an acceptance protocol for a
detector — what a cycle must survive before it is allowed to *mean* anything. Whether to put money
behind a detector that passes is Iddo's decision and a licensed advisor's, never mine.

## 1. מסקנה · THE RULING

**עברית — הטבע כן, הכלכלה כמעט לא, וההבדל אינו טעם אלא מספר.**
מחזור אמיתי נמדד **הרבה פעמים**. השעון הצירקדי נמדד באלפי מחזורים ויש לו מנגנון מולקולרי שזכה
בנובל; מחזור השמש נמדד ב־25 מחזורים מאז 1749; מילנקוביץ' נמדד בעשרות מחזורים בליבות ים.
**בכלכלה, המאמר הכי חזק שתומך בגל קונדרטייב מודה במילים שלו עצמו שהוא מדד "פחות משלושה מחזורים",
והמובהקות שלו היא 0.04 < p < 0.05.** זה לא ממצא — זה גודל מדגם של 2.6. גרוע מזה: הנדנדה של קוזנץ
הוכחה כ**תוצר של המסנן של קוזנץ עצמו** (Howrey 1968), והגלים של קונדרטייב הוכחו כתלויים בשיטת
ההחלקה שלו (Garvy 1943, אפקט Slutsky–Yule). ו־Granger הראה ב־1966 שהספקטרום הטיפוסי של משתנה
כלכלי הוא **חלק ויורד — בלי שיאים בכלל**. זה הרקע שכל טענת מחזור חייבת לנצח, ורובן לא.
**מה כן שורד:** ENSO — כי הוא נמדד במכשיר **מחוץ למחיר** (טמפרטורת פני הים), ויש לו מנגנון פיזיקלי.
אבל גם הוא משלם רק על ה**הפתעה**, כי החוזים העתידיים כבר מתמחרים את התחזית — והפתעה, בהגדרה,
אי אפשר לתזמן. **הכלל למערכת: אל תחפש מחזור בתוך הסדרה שאתה רוצה לסחור בה. חפש מתנד פיזיקלי
שנמדד בנפרד — ואז תבדוק אם השוק כבר יודע.**

**English — nature yes, economics almost not at all, and the difference is a number, not a taste.**
A real cycle is measured **many times over**. The circadian clock has thousands of cycles and a
Nobel-winning molecular mechanism; the solar cycle has ~25 cycles since 1749; Milankovitch has
tens of cycles in ocean cores. **In economics, the strongest paper supporting a Kondratieff wave
concedes, in its own words, that it measured "a period that does not exceed the length of three
cycles", at 0.04 < p < 0.05.** That is not a finding — that is n ≈ 2.6. Worse: the Kuznets swing
was shown to be **an artifact of Kuznets's own filter** (Howrey 1968), and Kondratieff's waves were
shown to depend on his smoothing method (Garvy 1943 — the Slutsky–Yule effect). And Granger showed
in 1966 that the typical spectrum of an economic variable is **smooth and declining, with no peaks
at all**. That is the background every cycle claim must beat, and almost none do.
**What survives:** ENSO — because it is measured by an instrument **outside the price** (sea
surface temperature) and has a physical mechanism. But even ENSO pays only on the **surprise**,
because futures already price the forecast — and a surprise, by definition, cannot be scheduled.
**The rule for the system: never mine a cycle out of the series you intend to trade. Find a
physical oscillator measured independently — then check whether the market already knows.**

## 2. Lens A — cycles in nature that are genuinely measured

| cycle | period | variance of the period | mechanism | grade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| circadian | **24.18 h** in humans | tight distribution, same in young and old | TTFL — transcription/translation negative feedback (PER/TIM) | **[M]** |
| circannual | **~300 days** free-running (10.5–11 months) | drifts out of season; needs a zeitgeber | endogenous, substrate not fully known | **[M]** |
| lynx–hare | **~10 yr** | 8–11 yr | **three trophic levels** — not two-species Lotka–Volterra | **[M]** cycle / **[F]** textbook mechanism |
| Milankovitch | 100 kyr, 41 kyr, ~19–23 kyr | astronomically exact | orbital insolation forcing | **[M]** forcing / **[C]** the 100-kyr response |
| solar (Schwabe) | **11.06 yr** mean | **9–14 yr** individually; ~14 yr in the Maunder Minimum, ~9 yr in the Medieval Maximum | solar dynamo; 22-yr Hale magnetic cycle underneath | **[M]** |
| ENSO | **2–7 yr, irregular** | broadband, not a line | Bjerknes positive feedback, delayed/recharge negative feedback, **stochastic** wind bursts | **[M]** as a noise-driven oscillator |
| periodical cicadas | **13 and 17 yr**, near-perfect synchrony | essentially zero | **unknown** — nobody knows how they count | **[M]** period / **[B]** the prime-number story |

**The circadian case is the methodological lesson of this whole ruling.** Before light exposure was
controlled, human "circadian periods" were reported ranging from **13 to 65 hours**. Czeisler et al.
(*Science* 1999) removed the confound and the true intrinsic period turned out to be **24.18 h with
a tight distribution**. A real cycle, badly measured, produced numbers spanning a factor of five.
**Bad measurement destroys a period estimate even when the cycle is genuinely there** — which is why
Lens C's tests are not pedantry, they are how you find out which case you are in.

**Circannual is the honest picture of what "a cycle" usually means.** Golden-mantled ground
squirrels kept for 2–4 years under constant light and temperature keep hibernating about once a
year — but at **~300 days**, not 365. The clock is endogenous and real; it is also **wrong by two
months a year** and needs the seasons to re-synchronise it. *A real biological cycle is a drifting
oscillator with a zeitgeber, not a metronome.*

**Lynx–hare is the cautionary tale.** The ~10-year Hudson's Bay Company record (1845–1937) is the
textbook illustration of Lotka–Volterra predator–prey oscillation — **and the textbook is wrong
about the mechanism.** Leigh (1968) and Gilpin (1973) showed the dynamic is not a two-species
predator–prey oscillation; much of it is intrinsic, density-dependent self-regulation. Krebs et al.
(*Science* 1995) then ran the real experiment: an 8-year factorial manipulation on 1 km² blocks in
the Yukon. **Predator exclosure doubled hare density. Food addition tripled it. Both together
raised it 11-fold** — more than additive, i.e. a three-trophic-level interaction. Added nutrients
grew plants but not hares. **So: the cycle was real for a century while the accepted explanation
was wrong.** A correct period is not evidence of a correct mechanism.

**Milankovitch is a measured forcing with a partly unexplained response.** Eccentricity ~100 kyr
(and ~413 kyr), obliquity ~41 kyr, precession ~25.8 kyr — found in deep-sea cores by Hays, Imbrie
and Shackleton (1976). But the **100,000-year problem** stands: the ~100-kyr glacial rhythm of the
last million years is driven by the component with the **weakest** insolation effect, and before
~1 Myr ago the dominant period was 41 kyr instead. The astronomy is exact; the climate's answer to
it is not fully derived. **[C]** on the response, **[M]** on the forcing.

**The solar cycle sets the realistic bar for "a good cycle".** Mean 11.06 yr over 23 cycles since
1749 — and **individual cycles run 9 to 14 years**, i.e. the period's spread is about plus/minus
20–25%. During the Maunder Minimum the mean length went to ~14 yr. **Anything in economics claiming
tighter regularity than the Sun is claiming more than the Sun delivers.**

**ENSO is not a clock, it is a damped oscillator kicked by noise.** Bjerknes positive feedback
amplifies an SST anomaly; a delayed oceanic feedback (wave reflection, recharge-discharge of
equatorial heat content) turns it around; **westerly wind bursts inject state-dependent randomness**.
That is why 2–7 years is a band and not a line, and why forecast skill converges at roughly **15–20
months** with a spring predictability barrier. **This matters enormously for Lens D**: a band-limited
oscillator lets you know *what state you are in*, not *what date the next turn is*.

**Cicadas: the period is the most exact in this table, and the explanation is the least settled.**
13 and 17 years, prime, with near-perfect mass synchrony. Competing accounts: predator-satiation
resonance against 2- and 3-year quasi-cyclic predators; avoidance of hybridisation between broods;
glacial cooling switching maturation from size-dependent to time-dependent. **The counting mechanism
is unknown.** Grade the number **[M]**, grade "because primes evade predators" **[B]** — it is a
plausible model, not a measurement.

## 3. Lens B — the named economic cycles, graded honestly

**The background fact first, because it decides everything.** Granger (*Econometrica* 1966), *The
Typical Spectral Shape of an Economic Variable*: economic series have spectra with mass concentrated
at low frequency, **declining smoothly with frequency — no peaks**. Reproduced since across dozens
of countries. So the null for any economic cycle claim is not "flat noise", it is **red noise with a
monotonically falling spectrum**, and a "peak" must beat *that*.

**And the arbiter does not claim a period.** The NBER dates turning points, not cycles. Since 1854
there have been about 34 cycles with mean contraction **17.0 months** and mean expansion **41.4
months** — but postwar expansions ran from **12 months (1980–81) to 128 months (2009–2020)**, and
contractions from **2 months (2020) to 18 months**. A spread of more than 10:1 is a **distribution
of durations**, not a period. Mankiw's textbook heads the section *"Economic Fluctuations Are
Irregular and Unpredictable"* — a line quoted, notably, inside the leading **pro**-Kondratieff paper.

| named cycle | claimed period | best evidence FOR | evidence AGAINST | grade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Kitchin** (inventory) | 3–5 yr | reduced spectrum of world GDP, 3–4 yr, **p about 0.02–0.03** (Korotayev and Tsirel 2010) | frequency was **searched, not pre-registered**; Granger's declining spectrum; no independent replication with a red-noise null found | **[C]**, leaning **[B]** |
| **Juglar** (fixed investment) | 7–11 yr | same paper, 7–9 yr, **p about 0.02–0.03** | same objection; NBER durations are 10:1 dispersed | **[C]** |
| **Kuznets** (building/demographic) | 15–25 yr | Kuznets (1930), Abramovitz (1961) | **Howrey (1968): Kuznets's own filter amplified the 20-year component more than threefold.** The swing is substantially an artifact of the data processing | **[F]** as originally measured |
| **Kondratieff** (long waves) | 45–60 yr | Korotayev and Tsirel 2010: ~52–53 yr in world GDP 1870–2007, **0.04 < p < 0.05**; Hecht 2023/2025 unobserved-component models, ~50 yr, 25 series | **Garvy (1943)**: the waves are "in part at least" a product of the techniques used, turning points arbitrarily dated, data limited in time and scope. **Failed detections: van Ewijk (1982), Metz (1998, 2006), Diebolt and Doliger (2006, 2008), Solomou (1988, 1990).** Metz **renounced his own earlier positive findings** | **[B]** — believed, not established |

### The single most important paragraph in this ruling

The **strongest statistical case ever made for Kondratieff waves** states its own limit in its own
words. Korotayev and Tsirel (2010), world GDP 1870–2007, detect ~52–53 years and write that p
between 0.04 and 0.05 characterises

> "…the degree of our confidence that we have managed to detect K-waves with spectral analysis on
> the basis of series of observations that cover a period that does not exceed the length of three
> cycles."

**137 years divided by 52.5 years = 2.6 cycles.** They then argue that p < 0.05 is "generally
regarded to be sufficient". **It is not — not at n = 2.6, not with a bespoke significance method
built for the paper, and not for a frequency that was found by searching.** If Iddo's system
accepted this standard it would accept almost anything.

**And the same paper's own appendix proves the point against itself.** In its worked example, the
best candidate periodicity has a significance of **35%** when the frequency is searched for — but
**0.17% (p = 0.0017) if the same frequency had been proposed in advance.** That is a factor of
roughly **200x** between "I found it" and "I predicted it". The authors state this plainly. It is
the multiple-comparisons problem, admitted by the proponents, and it is the origin of Gate G2 below.

**The pro side, stated fairly (dissent, kept visible).** Hecht (*Review of Political Economy* 2025,
37(4):1681–1715), *Are Long Waves 50 Years?*, replicates Kondratieff's published models and applies
unobserved-component models to his original data plus new long-run Bank of England series across 25
economic and financial series — and reports a confirmed endogenously-propagated long cycle of about
fifty years. **[C]** — but with a standing objection: an unobserved-component model with a cyclical
component **imposes** a cycle by construction and then estimates its period; it does not test whether
a cycle exists against a red-noise null. I could not read the full text this session (section 10).

**Nelson and Plosser (1982)** examined 14 US macro series and found most consistent with a stochastic
trend — a random walk with drift — rather than a deterministic trend with cyclical deviations. If
true, there is no fixed trend to cycle *around*. This has been contested from a Bayesian direction
and on revised data, so grade it **[C]** — but the contest itself is the point: **the cyclical
component of macro data is not cleanly identified even by the people arguing about it.**

## 4. Lens C — the statistics of finding a cycle that is not there

**This is the most important lens, and it is almost a century old.**

### 4.1 A cycle can be manufactured out of pure randomness

**Slutsky (1927/1937)**, *The Summation of Random Causes as the Source of Cyclic Processes*, took
**random numbers drawn from a public lottery**, applied a moving average, and produced a series with
the characteristics of a macroeconomic business cycle. **Yule (1927)** showed an AR(2) driven by
white noise produces cycles *of such regularity that one might imagine a mechanical origin*.
**A moving average of noise looks exactly like a business cycle. That is not an analogy — it is a
theorem with a demonstration.**

This is not history. It is the direct explanation of two of the four named economic cycles:
- **Howrey (1968)** — the Kuznets long swing: **the filter amplified the ~20-year component more
  than threefold.** The swing was largely made by the smoothing.
- **Garvy (1943)** — Kondratieff's moving-average detrending, read against Slutsky and Yule: the
  waves are at least in part the technique's, not the economy's.

### 4.2 Modern filters do the same thing, and the field still uses them

**Hamilton (2018)**, *Why You Should Never Use the Hodrick-Prescott Filter*, REStat 100(5):831–843:
the HP filter **"produces series with spurious dynamic relations that have no basis in the underlying
data-generating process"**; end-of-sample values are the worst affected; and a proper statistical
formalisation gives smoothing parameters **far below the conventional lambda = 1600**. He proposes
regressing the variable at *t+h* on its four most recent values as of *t* instead. (Contested —
published comments argue his alternative carries some of the same drawbacks — but the diagnosis of
HP is not seriously disputed.) **Consequence for the build: no HP filter, no band-pass, no moving
average before the periodicity test. If the cycle only appears after filtering, it is the filter's
cycle.**

### 4.3 The peak you found competes against every peak you could have found

**Spurious peaks are large.** Frescura, Engelbrecht and Frank (2007) state it flatly: noise produces
"surprisingly large" spurious peaks that reflect no periodicity at all. The correct false-alarm
probability over N independent frequencies is

    FAP = 1 - [ F_Z(z) ]^N

so the threshold for a *searched* peak is vastly higher than for a *pre-specified* one. Two further
findings from that paper matter directly:
- **Over-sampling the periodogram inflates the apparent number of independent frequencies** and
  corrupts the test. They failed to reproduce Horne and Baliunas's widely used quadratic relation
  between independent frequencies and sample size, and conjecture it is an artifact of gross
  over-sampling.
- **The theoretical false-alarm functions fit the empirical distributions badly** in exactly the
  region that matters. Their recommendation: **a Monte Carlo test tailored to the actual series**.

**And the classical test assumes the wrong noise.** Fisher's exact *g*-test assumes **Gaussian white
noise**; Whittle noted the requirement in 1952. Economic and geophysical series are **red**. Testing
a red series against a white null is how you generate a career of false cycles. The right procedure
for this exact situation is the astronomers': Vaughan (2005), *A simple test for periodic signals in
red noise* — cited, notably, by the Kondratieff paper itself.

### 4.4 The general-purpose weapon: surrogate data

**Theiler et al. (1992)**, *Testing for nonlinearity in time series: the method of surrogate data*.
State a linear null; generate surrogates that satisfy it by **randomising the Fourier phases and
inverting** — so each surrogate has **exactly the same power spectrum and autocorrelation** as the
real series but no genuine phase structure; compute the discriminating statistic on the data and on
the ensemble; reject only if the data lie outside the ensemble. For non-Gaussian series use IAAFT
(amplitude-adjusted) surrogates. **This is the test that makes "is this peak real?" an empirical
question instead of an argument.**

### 4.5 The n = 2 problem, stated as arithmetic

Over a record of length *T*, a claimed period *P* gives **n = T/P realisations of the cycle**. Every
statement about the *stability* of a period is a statement about a sample of size n.

| claim | best record | n | what that n means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kondratieff 52.5 yr | 137 yr (1870–2007) | **2.6** | you have seen it twice. You cannot estimate a variance from two numbers |
| Kuznets 20 yr | 137 yr | 6.9 | and Howrey showed the filter made most of it |
| Juglar 9 yr | 137 yr | 15 | enough observations; still fails pre-registration |
| Kitchin 3.5 yr | 137 yr | 39 | enough observations; the peak is weak against a red null |
| solar 11 yr | 275 yr | 25 | this is what an accepted cycle looks like |
| circadian 24.18 h | months | thousands | this is what a *settled* cycle looks like |

**A 50-year cycle observed over 100 years of data has n = 2. There is no statistical procedure that
rescues n = 2, and no amount of methodological sophistication substitutes for observations.**

### 4.6 The finance-specific failures, by name

- **Jevons (1875–1878), the sunspot theory of commercial crises — the founding example of curve
  fitting.** He observed roughly 10-year crises and wrote that he could see no reason for the human
  mind to select "a period of just 10.44 years". His astronomer friends gave the solar period as
  **10.45 years** — and **he adjusted his dating of the commercial crises to fit**, later conceding
  his crisis dating was suspect and that crises recurred *more* regularly than sunspot extrema. No
  systematic relation was found; some crises fell at sunspot **maxima**. Garcia-Mata and Shaffner
  (1934) found no support. **Grade [F].** The mechanism was plausible (sunspots to weather to crops
  to demand). Plausibility is not evidence.
- **Multiple testing across the whole factor literature.** Harvey, Liu and Zhu (*Review of Financial
  Studies* 2016, 29(1):5–68): **at least 316 factors** have been tested; the conventional t > 2.0 is
  the wrong cutoff; **a newly discovered factor needs t > 3.0**. Their own summary is that most
  claimed findings in financial economics are likely false.
- **Backtest overfitting is quantifiable.** Bailey, Borwein, Lopez de Prado and Zhu,
  *Pseudo-Mathematics and Financial Charlatanism*, *Notices of the AMS* 61(5):458–471 — the
  **Probability of Backtest Overfitting (PBO)** via combinatorially symmetric cross-validation, the
  **Deflated Sharpe Ratio** (corrects for selection under multiple testing and non-normal returns),
  and the **Minimum Backtest Length**.
- **Leakage in validation.** Ordinary k-fold on overlapping financial labels leaks the future
  backwards. **Purging plus embargo** (Lopez de Prado) is the minimum; combinatorial purged CV is
  better.
- **Published anomalies decay.** McLean and Pontiff (*Journal of Finance* 2016), 97 predictors:
  **26% lower out-of-sample, 58% lower post-publication.**
- **Seasonality that vanished when properly tested.** Dichtl and Drobetz's true out-of-sample test
  of the **Halloween effect**: since **January 2003** — after the seminal international study was
  published — the effect is **not evident at 95% confidence for any index**. The **January effect**
  declined sharply after 1987–88 and is argued to have disappeared statistically for the major US
  indices (Mehdian and Perry 2002; Gu 2003) — though contested by work saying it merely reverted to
  pre-1963 levels rather than dying. Grade the January effect **[C]**, Halloween **[F] out of
  sample**.

## 5. Lens D — where cycles genuinely do reach markets

**Be strict here. Two things are true at once and both must be said.**

### 5.1 ENSO to commodity prices is real, and it is the best honest candidate

| study | design | result |
|---|---|---|
| **Brunner (2002)**, REStat 84(1):176–183 | VAR on an ENSO index vs world real commodity prices | a **one-SD El Nino surprise raises real commodity price inflation about 3.5–4 percentage points**; ENSO accounts for **over 20%** of commodity price inflation movements, and **10–15%** of world CPI inflation and world activity |
| **Cashin, Mohaddes and Raissi (2017)**, JIE 106:37–54 | GVAR, 21 country/region models, 1979Q2–2013Q1, direct plus third-market transmission | effects **differ by country**; strongest on inflation and interest rates; short-run oil price rise |
| **Ubilava**, TV-STAR | Nino3.4 SST vs **46 commodity prices**, monthly, Jan 1982 – Dec 2015 | **vegetable oils most responsive: up to +5 pp after an El Nino surprise, apparent after about one year; ~15% of variance at 1 yr, up to a third at 2 yr.** Wheat **+2 pp after La Nina** (~25% of variance). Arabica coffee **over +2% after La Nina** (~15%). Fishmeal +3%, salmon +2%, soybean meal -2%, beef -0.5% |

**Why this is the strongest candidate:** the oscillator is measured by **an instrument outside the
price series** (sea surface temperature in the Nino3.4 region), the transmission is a supply
mechanism you can name (water for irrigation, hydro power for mining, fisheries), and the effect
sizes converge across three independent methods and four decades.

### 5.2 The four things that make it much weaker than it looks — from the authors themselves

1. **Negative results inside the same study.** Ubilava reports that **barley, maize and sorghum show
   no causal linkage with ENSO** — regional diversification of production cancels it. Losses in one
   region are offset by gains in another.
2. **The effect is decaying.** Several series show **structural change with the ENSO effect
   mitigated over time**, which the author reads as **adaptation** — the economy learning. An effect
   that shrinks as it becomes known is exactly the McLean-Pontiff pattern in a physical dress.
3. **Statistically significant is not economically meaningful, and the paper says so.** Gold prices
   rise **"statistically significantly by approximately a tenth of a percentage point"** after
   El Nino — the author's own comment is that this is not economically meaningful. **46 commodities
   were tested; at alpha = 0.05 you expect about 2.3 false positives by construction.** The strong
   results (vegetable oils, wheat) are well clear of that; the marginal ones are precisely what
   multiplicity manufactures.
4. **The market already prices the forecast.** Wheat, soybean and corn futures decline after an
   El Nino **forecast**; near-term vegetable-oil futures incorporate El Nino supply risk in advance,
   and traders priced the 2026 event **before** the official advisory. NOAA publishes ENSO forecasts
   publicly, monthly, for free.

**Therefore the honest Lens D verdict:** every published ENSO-price result is estimated on
**shocks, surprises, unanticipated deviations** — that is the modelling choice in Brunner, in
Cashin et al., and in Ubilava. **A surprise is, by construction, the part nobody could forecast.**
The cycle is real, the transmission is real, the effect size is real — **and the exploitable residual
is the part that was unpredictable.** That is not a contradiction in the research; it is the research
telling you honestly what it measured.

**Grade: [M] for the physical link. [B] for "and therefore it is tradeable on a schedule."**

### 5.3 Seasonality: what survived and what did not

- **Vanished:** the Halloween effect fails a true out-of-sample test after its own publication date
  (not significant at 95% for any index since Jan 2003). **[F]**
- **Faded, contested:** the January / small-firm effect — a pronounced declining trend since 1988,
  argued dead for the major indices by some, argued merely reverted by others. **[C]**
- **The general law:** 26% out-of-sample decay, 58% post-publication decay across 97 predictors.
  **Any calendar effect must be assumed at least 58% smaller live than in the paper.**

## 6. THE FOUR ANSWERS

### 6.1 Which cycles are real, measured, and mechanistically explained?

**Circadian** (24.18 h, TTFL, Nobel 2017) · **circannual** (~300 d free-running, needs a zeitgeber) ·
**the solar Schwabe cycle** (11.06 yr mean, 9–14 yr spread, dynamo plus the 22-yr Hale cycle) ·
**Milankovitch forcing** (100/41/23 kyr, astronomically exact — the *response* at 100 kyr is not
fully explained) · **ENSO** (2–7 yr band, Bjerknes plus delayed feedback plus stochastic wind
bursts — a noise-driven oscillator, not a clock) · **the hare cycle** (~10 yr — real, and its
accepted two-species mechanism was wrong for sixty years until an 8-year field experiment produced
the three-trophic answer) · **periodical cicadas** (13/17 yr — the most exact period in the whole
ruling, with the least settled explanation).

### 6.2 Which are claimed but not distinguishable from noise — by name?

- **Kondratieff long waves (~50 yr) — [B].** Best supporting evidence is p between 0.04 and 0.05 at
  **n = 2.6 cycles** with a bespoke significance method and a searched frequency. Failed detections
  by van Ewijk, Metz (who renounced his own earlier positive result), Diebolt and Doliger, Solomou.
  Garvy showed technique-dependence in 1943.
- **Kuznets swings (15–25 yr) — [F] as originally established.** Howrey (1968): the filter amplified
  the 20-year component more than threefold.
- **Kitchin and Juglar — [C], leaning [B].** They have enough observations (n about 39 and 15) to be
  testable, which is more than Kondratieff can say — but the only significance numbers found
  (p about 0.02–0.03) come from a **searched** frequency in the same paper, and Granger's declining
  spectrum is the standing counter-evidence.
- **Jevons's sunspot theory of business cycles — [F].** The founding curve-fit.
- **The Halloween effect — [F] out of sample.** **The January effect — [C], badly faded.**

## 7. The three failure modes this ruling exists to prevent

1. **You will find a cycle. Everyone does.** Slutsky found one in lottery draws. The question is
   never "is there a peak" — there is always a peak — but "is this peak bigger than the biggest peak
   noise would have produced anywhere in the band I searched."
2. **The filter you use to see the cycle is usually what made it.** HP, moving averages, band-pass —
   all install oscillations in noise. Two of the four named economic cycles are substantially filter
   artifacts.
3. **A correct period does not certify a mechanism, and a plausible mechanism does not certify a
   period.** The hare cycle had the right period and the wrong mechanism for sixty years. Jevons had
   a beautiful mechanism and no cycle.

## 8. שער המחזור · THE CYCLE GATE — the acceptance criterion

**Contract:** a claimed periodicity is **רלוונטי / relevant** only if it passes **all ten gates**.
Any failure means **לא רלוונטי**, and the failure is recorded with the claim, by name. Per PLAN
section 2.9, every ruling ships with its dissent attached.

| # | gate | pass condition | why — the source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G0** | **Exogenous instrument** | the oscillator is measured by an instrument **outside** the series you intend to act on (SST, sunspot number, the calendar, an orbital ephemeris). If the only evidence is a peak in the target series itself, the prior is **fail**, and G1–G9 must all pass at their strict settings | ENSO passes; Kondratieff cannot — it is mined from GDP to be used on GDP |
| **G1** | **Enough cycles** | **n >= 8 complete cycles in-sample, plus >= 2 complete cycles held out and never touched.** Total span >= 10 periods. n = T_years / P_years, computed and checked **before** anything else runs | Korotayev's own "does not exceed three cycles" at p about 0.045 |
| **G2** | **Pre-register the frequency** | period, phase, direction, horizon and the exact test written to a file, hashed and timestamped, **before** the test runs | that same paper's appendix: **35% searched vs 0.17% pre-specified — a ~200x difference** |
| **G3** | **Red-noise null, never white** | fit AR(1) or a bending power law to the series' own spectrum; test the peak against **that** | Granger 1966; Whittle 1952; Vaughan 2005 |
| **G4** | **Surrogate test with the search built in** | >= 1000 phase-randomised surrogates (IAAFT if non-Gaussian). **Statistic = the MAXIMUM periodogram power over the entire searched band**, not the power at the winning frequency. Empirical p = (1 + number of surrogates >= observed) / (1 + N_surr). **Require p <= 0.01** | Theiler et al. 1992; FAP = 1 - F(z)^N; Frescura et al. recommend exactly this Monte Carlo route |
| **G5** | **No filter before the test** | no HP, no moving average, no band-pass. Detrend by first differences or Hamilton's regression-on-four-lags. Do **not** over-sample the periodogram | Slutsky/Yule; Howrey 1968; Hamilton 2018; Frescura et al. on over-sampling |
| **G6** | **Phase must predict, not fit** | estimate period and phase on the first half; **forecast the turning points of the untouched second half**; require phase error under one quarter of a period on **every** held-out cycle. **Re-dating turning points to save the fit is an automatic, permanent fail** | Jevons moved the crisis dates to match 10.45 yr; Garvy found Kondratieff's turning points arbitrary |
| **G7** | **Mechanism plus instrument plus kill condition** | state what physically produces it, what instrument measures that thing, and **what observation would end the claim** | the hare cycle had the period and the wrong mechanism for 60 years |
| **G8** | **Significance after the haircuts** | report how many specifications were tried. Require **t > 3.0** (Harvey-Liu-Zhu), **Deflated Sharpe Ratio > 0**, **PBO < 5%** (Bailey et al.), on **purged and embargoed walk-forward CV** (Lopez de Prado). Then **discount expected live return by 58%** before sizing anything | Harvey/Liu/Zhu 2016; Bailey et al. 2014; McLean and Pontiff 2016 |
| **G9** | **Already-priced check** | is the oscillator publicly forecast (NOAA ENSO advisories, sunspot forecasts, the calendar)? If yes, the only admissible signal is the **surprise** relative to the public forecast — and you must show that surprise is not itself forecastable from public information | ENSO forecasts are free, monthly, and already in the futures curve |

### 8.1 The gate applied — the table the detector must reproduce

| claim | period | n available | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| circadian | 24.18 h | thousands | **PASS all** |
| solar Schwabe | 11.06 yr | ~25 since 1749 | **PASS G0–G5, G7.** G6 loose: 20–25% period spread |
| Milankovitch | 100/41/23 kyr | tens | **PASS G0–G6.** G7 partial — the 100-kyr response is unexplained |
| cicadas | 13/17 yr | ~20 brood emergences on record | **PASS G6 spectacularly** (phase is exact). **FAIL G7** — mechanism unknown |
| lynx–hare | ~10 yr | ~9 over the HBC record | **FAIL G1** (marginal). Mechanism settled by an 8-year manipulation, not by the time series |
| ENSO | 2–7 yr | ~35 over 140 yr of SST | **PASS G0, G1, G3, G4, G7. FAIL G6** — it is a band, not a phase. Usable as a **state**, never as a **date** |
| Kitchin | 3–5 yr | ~39 | PASS G1. **FAIL G2** (searched); G4 unproven against red noise |
| Juglar | 7–11 yr | ~15 | PASS G1. **FAIL G2, G6** — NBER durations are 10:1 dispersed |
| Kuznets | 15–25 yr | ~7 | **FAIL G1, FAIL G5** (Howrey: the filter made it) |
| **Kondratieff** | 45–60 yr | **2.6** | **FAIL G0, G1, G2, G5, G6.** Five gates. The clearest fail in the table |
| January effect | 1 yr | ~100 | PASS G1, G6. **FAIL G8** — decayed after publication |
| Halloween effect | 1 yr | ~100 | PASS G1, G6. **FAIL** true out-of-sample since 2003 |
| **ENSO to vegetable oils / wheat** | 2–7 yr | ~8.5 (1982–2015) | **PASS G0, G3, G7.** G1 marginal. **FAIL G9** — futures already price the public forecast; only the surprise remains, and the surprise is unforecastable by construction |

**Read the last column honestly: no economic periodicity in this table passes the gate as a clock,
and the one physical driver that reaches markets pays only on its unforecastable component.**

### 8.2 What the detector should actually be built to do instead

Do not build a period-finder for price series. Build **three** things, in this order:

1. **A gate implementation** — G1 through G9 as a callable `assess(series, claimed_period, ...)`
   returning a ruling plus its dissent, with the surrogate ensemble as its core. **Its first job is
   to reproduce section 8.1**, including passing circadian and solar and failing Kondratieff and
   Kuznets. **If it cannot reproduce the known failures, it is not ready to judge anything new.**
   That is the acceptance test *for the detector itself* — the control that PLAN section 5 requires
   of every experiment in this project.
2. **An exogenous-oscillator registry** — physical processes with independent instruments (Nino3.4
   SST, sunspot number, orbital ephemerides, harvest calendars), each carrying its measured period,
   its **period variance**, its forecast horizon, and **whether a public forecast exists** (G9).
3. **A surprise extractor** — for any registered oscillator with a public forecast, the only signal
   is `observed - publicly_forecast`. Everything else was priced before you looked.

## 9. What would make this ruling wrong — the standing challenges

**Written now, so it cannot be softened later. Each is concrete and checkable.**

1. **Kondratieff flips if it predicts.** Hecht's ~50-year estimate implies a **dated next turning
   point**. Write that date down today, pre-registered (G2), and check it when it arrives. A
   pre-registered, out-of-sample, correctly dated turn on a series Kondratieff never saw would flip
   this ruling's central verdict. **Nothing else will.**
2. **Kondratieff also flips on n.** If someone assembles a genuinely independent record long enough
   to give **n >= 8** at ~50 years — 400+ years of comparable data, which does not currently exist
   for world GDP — the arithmetic objection dissolves and the question becomes empirical again.
3. **My unobserved-component objection could itself be wrong.** I argue UC models impose a cycle by
   construction. If a UC specification is shown to *reject* a cyclical component when one is absent
   from synthetic red noise of matched spectrum, that objection fails and Hecht's evidence upgrades
   from **[C]** toward **[M]**. **This is a one-afternoon experiment and I did not run it.**
4. **Lens D flips if a pre-registered ENSO rule beats the curve.** Specify the rule now; run it
   forward across **at least two further ENSO events**; if it beats the futures curve out of sample
   after costs, "already priced" is wrong and section 5.2 item 4 falls.
5. **G1's threshold is a judgement, not a theorem.** n >= 8 is a proxy for signal-to-noise. A very
   strong, low-noise periodicity can be validly established at n = 3. **If someone produces a formal
   power calculation for the specific series, it supersedes G1** — and G1 should be replaced by that
   calculation rather than defended.
6. **I may be over-reading the Metz renunciation.** I have it from a secondary source (Korotayev and
   Tsirel's literature review) and did not read Metz 1998/2006 directly. If the retraction is
   narrower than reported, the "failed detections" column weakens.

**עברית —** בטבע יש מחזורים אמיתיים, ומה שהופך אותם לאמיתיים הוא שנמדדו **הרבה פעמים** ויש להם

(המחזור השנתי). בכלכלה כמעט שום דבר לא עובר את הרף הזה: קונדרטייב נמדד על **2.6 מחזורים** ב־p של
0.045, קוזנץ הוכח כתוצר של המסנן של עצמו, וג'בונס הזיז את תאריכי המשברים כדי שיתאימו לשמש. הדבר
היחיד שבאמת מגיע לשווקים הוא ENSO — כי מודדים אותו במכשיר שמחוץ למחיר — ואפילו הוא משלם רק על
ההפתעה, כי את התחזית השוק כבר תימחר. **הכלל אחד: אל תחפש מחזור בתוך הסדרה שאתה רוצה לפעול עליה;
מצא מתנד שנמדד בנפרד, ותרשום את התדר לפני שאתה מסתכל.**

**English —** Nature has real cycles, and what makes them real is that they were measured **many
times** and have a mechanism you can measure separately — and even the best of them wander by
20–25% (the Sun) or drift two months a year (circannual). Almost nothing in economics clears that
bar: Kondratieff rests on **2.6 cycles** at p about 0.045, Kuznets was made by its own filter, and
Jevons moved the crisis dates to fit the Sun. The one thing that genuinely reaches markets is ENSO —
because it is measured by an instrument outside the price — and even ENSO pays only on the surprise,
because the market has already priced the forecast. **One rule: never mine a cycle from the series
you intend to act on; find an independently measured oscillator, and write the frequency down before
you look.**

**מסקנה:** בטבע — כן, ובמנגנון מוכח; בכלכלה — כמעט שום דבר לא שורד את עדשה ג', קונדרטייב וקוזנץ נופלים במפורש, והשער היחיד שמותר לסמוך עליו הוא: מתנד חיצוני שנמדד בנפרד, תדר שנרשם מראש, מבחן סורוגייט מול רעש אדום, ושני מחזורים שמעולם לא נגעת בהם.
