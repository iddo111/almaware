# ALMA Public-Benefit Charter

## Purpose

ALMA, Eran, Perets, Yigael, and related prototypes exist to create measurable
public benefit. The project is larger than any founder, personality, political
claim, or religious interpretation. No claim of destiny or special authority
can replace evidence, consent, law, or independent review.

## Non-negotiable commitments

1. **Voluntary participation.** People and communities choose whether to join,
   can understand the terms, and can leave without punishment or loss of basic
   services.
2. **Human rights and democratic choice.** The project does not overthrow,
   coerce, manipulate, or govern populations. It preserves lawful institutions,
   civil liberties, pluralism, and the right to disagree.
3. **Non-weaponization.** Robots, models, data, and infrastructure are not
   designed for weapons, targeting, repression, forced compliance, or covert
   surveillance of individuals.
4. **Community control.** Local partners retain meaningful ownership of their
   data, tools, infrastructure, and operating decisions. Expansion requires
   explicit local approval.
5. **Transparent governance.** Funding, objectives, known risks, incidents,
   metrics, and material conflicts of interest are documented in accessible
   language. Material failures are not hidden.
6. **Independent safety review.** A reviewer who is not controlled by the
   project can stop or restrict a pilot. Retaliation against reviewers,
   participants, whistleblowers, or critics is prohibited.
7. **Evidence before scale.** Every pilot defines a beneficiary, baseline,
   measurable outcome, harm indicators, owner, time limit, and stop condition.
   A compelling story is not acceptance evidence.
8. **Bounded automation.** Agents and robots have explicit scopes, quotas,
   audit logs, and human stop controls. They may not recruit workers, replicate,
   spend money, acquire infrastructure, or expand territory without a separately
   recorded authorization.
9. **Responsible health claims.** Medicinal-plant work is educational or
   horticultural unless qualified clinical and legal review supports a specific
   health claim. It never replaces emergency or professional medical care.
10. **Environmental responsibility.** Material, energy, water, waste, and
    biodiversity effects are measured. A project cannot claim ecological good
    by exporting its harms elsewhere.

## Pilot acceptance gate

A pilot may start only when all hard gates in `alma_govern.public_benefit` pass.
Approval expires at the pilot's declared end date. Scaling is a new decision,
not an automatic continuation.

A pilot succeeds only when its predeclared benefit target is met, serious-harm
limits are not exceeded, participants can confirm meaningful consent, and an
independent reviewer accepts the evidence. Otherwise its status is `PAUSE` or
`REJECT`, never “successful by intention.”

## First four tracks

| Track | Bounded deliverable | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Governance | This charter plus executable proposal gate | Tests cover every hard commitment |
| Safe hardware | One non-weaponized memristor/robotics prototype | Compile, bench protocol, electrical safety review, measured result |
| Community pilot | One opt-in food, ecology, education, disaster, music, or garden pilot | Local approval, baseline, outcome, harm and withdrawal records |
| Independent review | Named external reviewer and public evidence packet | Signed decision, disclosed conflicts, unresolved objections recorded |
