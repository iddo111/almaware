# ALMA organization protection packet

Draft date: 2026-07-22  
Jurisdiction assumption: Israel  
Status: internal design draft; **not legal, tax, medical, aviation, or financial advice**

This packet translates the ALMA Public-Benefit Charter into distinct bodies,
decision funnels, correction paths, and high-standard checklists. It protects
beneficiaries, communities, the organization, and the founder from concentrated
power, rushed decisions, conflicts of interest, hidden harm, and mission drift.

No political or religious identity gives anyone authority in this structure.
Authority comes from a defined role, documented evidence, consent, review, and
revocable decisions.

## 1. Minimum viable structure

Do not create many legal entities at once. Begin with documented functions;
incorporate only after an Israeli lawyer and accountant confirm the purpose,
tax, liability, employment, intellectual-property, and reporting design.

| Body | Purpose | May do | May not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial operating company | Build and sell safe products/services; employ technical staff | R&D, contracts, paid services, manufacturing after approval | Control charitable donations, claim charitable status, weaponize systems |
| Public-benefit body | Community pilots, education, hunger/ecology/disaster work | Grants, donations and volunteer programs after proper registration and controls | Subsidize insiders or the company without an arm's-length, mission-valid agreement |
| Independent safety and ethics council | Review high-risk pilots, incidents, conflicts and scaling | Require evidence, pause a pilot, publish a dissent | Operate products, receive sales commission, be removed for an adverse finding |
| Community partner council | Represent the people affected by a pilot | Approve local scope, data use, withdrawal and benefit metrics | Be treated as ceremonial consent or overruled by a founder alone |
| Finance and audit function | Budget, restricted funds, receipts, related parties and reporting | Reconcile, report, stop unsupported payment | Report solely to the person initiating payments |
| Privacy/data protection function | Data minimization, notices, access, retention, incidents | Refuse unnecessary collection and stop unsafe processing | Reuse data because it is technically accessible |

After professional review, the public-benefit body might be an association
(*amutah*) or a public-benefit company (*חל״צ*). Israel's Corporations Authority
provides a registration service for both; this draft does not select or register
either. [Official registration service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/association_registration)

## 2. Separation rules

1. Separate bank accounts, bookkeeping, contracts, data stores and governing
   minutes for commercial and charitable work.
2. Charitable money is restricted to the stated public purpose. It is not a
   founder account, emergency company financing, or payment for undocumented
   intellectual property.
3. Related-party transactions require disclosure, recusal, written need,
   comparable terms, finance review, and independent approval.
4. Intellectual-property ownership and licenses are written before a pilot.
   Community-owned data and locally built infrastructure cannot be silently
   absorbed into company assets.
5. Branding must distinguish product claims, research hypotheses, charity
   programs, personal beliefs, fiction, and independently verified results.
6. A person may serve in more than one body only when counsel approves the role
   and the conflict/recusal procedure remains effective.

## 3. Decision funnel

```text
Idea
  -> identity and scope record
  -> ALMA public-benefit JSON gate
  -> legal / privacy / conflict / medical / aviation / hazard screens
  -> affected-community review and withdrawable consent
  -> independent safety review
  -> bounded budget and two-person authorization
  -> time-limited pilot with baseline and stop conditions
  -> public evidence packet and incident window
  -> independent outcome decision
  -> close, correct, repeat, or submit a new scale request
```

No step can be skipped because the idea feels urgent, historically significant,
spiritually meaningful, obviously benevolent, or technically impressive.

## 4. Authority and founder protection

- The founder proposes vision and may lead technical work, but cannot alone
  approve high-risk deployment, related-party payment, medical claim, public
  fundraising, drone flight, autonomous replication, or beneficiary-data use.
- Set a board-approved spending threshold (`[BOARD-SET]`). Payments above it
  need two authorized people; payments to insiders always need independent
  review regardless of amount.
- Use a 24-hour cooling-off period for public claims, purchases, contracts, or
  deployments above the routine threshold; use 72 hours for irreversible or
  high-risk actions.
- Do not make major organizational decisions during severe sleep loss, unusual
  urgency, intoxication, acute distress, or a period of unusually intense
  certainty. Record the draft, pause, sleep, and obtain review from a trusted
  person who is empowered to disagree.
- Keep a private support list: trusted relative/friend, physician or therapist,
  lawyer, accountant, and operational deputy. Organizational safeguards are not
  mental-health treatment.
- If thoughts become overwhelming, sleep disappears, or there is pressure to
  confront someone or act immediately, stop operations and contact a trusted
  person or health professional before continuing.

## 5. High-standard launch checklist

### Purpose and governance

- [ ] One-sentence public purpose names beneficiaries and measurable benefit.
- [ ] Charter adopted by all operating bodies.
- [ ] Founders, directors, officers and beneficial interests disclosed.
- [ ] Conflict, recusal, whistleblower, complaint and appeal policies adopted.
- [ ] Independent reviewer has written stop authority and protected tenure.
- [ ] Minutes record approvals, objections, recusals and unresolved risks.
- [ ] Lawyer selects entity form; accountant approves bookkeeping/tax design.

### Money and fundraising

- [ ] Separate accounts and accounting ledgers exist.
- [ ] Two-person payment approval and bank reconciliation are configured.
- [ ] Restricted donations are tracked separately.
- [ ] No tax-deductibility claim is made without a valid Section 46 approval.
- [ ] Donation receipts and reporting workflow are tested before fundraising.
- [ ] Salaries, expenses, grants and related-party contracts are documented.
- [ ] Twelve-month conservative budget and shutdown reserve are approved.

From 1 January 2026, public institutions holding Section 46 approval must report
all donations through the Israel Tax Authority's Israel Donations system.
[Official donation-reporting service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/report-public-institution-receipt-donation)

### Privacy and knowledge base

- [ ] Data inventory states purpose, source, fields, owner, users and location.
- [ ] Collect the minimum data; avoid sensitive data when the benefit works
  without it.
- [ ] Plain-language notice, lawful basis/consent and withdrawal path exist.
- [ ] Participant access, correction, deletion and retention processes are tested.
- [ ] Role-based access, MFA, encryption, backups and incident logs are verified.
- [ ] Cross-border/cloud transfers are documented and reviewed.
- [ ] Serious security incidents have an escalation and official-reporting path.

Israel's Privacy Protection Authority states that Amendment 13 narrowed the
categories requiring database registration, but databases outside those
categories remain subject to purpose limitation, confidentiality, security and
data-subject rights. [Official registration guidance](https://www.gov.il/he/service/registration_in_the_database)

### AI, agents, robotics and drones

- [ ] Exact system/model/firmware identity and owner are recorded.
- [ ] Non-weaponization and no-coercive-surveillance tests pass.
- [ ] Tools, permissions, quotas, network destinations and spending are bounded.
- [ ] Human stop control works without cloud access.
- [ ] Logs are readable, retained, privacy-filtered and independently reviewable.
- [ ] No self-replication, worker recruitment or scale without a new approval.
- [ ] Physical voltage/current/thermal/mechanical checks precede actuation.
- [ ] Drone registration, operator, airspace, location, insurance and emergency
  procedures are verified with current aviation rules before any flight.

### Food, seeds, chemistry and medicinal plants

- [ ] Physical inventory records substance/species, amount, location and owner.
- [ ] Safety data, storage compatibility, labeling and disposal are reviewed.
- [ ] Seeds/plants are identified and locally legal; invasive/ecological risks
  are checked.
- [ ] Food handling and community-garden responsibilities are assigned.
- [ ] Education/horticulture is separated from diagnosis and treatment claims.
- [ ] Any health claim receives qualified clinical, toxicology and legal review.
- [ ] Emergency or professional care is never replaced by a project recommendation.

### Communications and public claims

- [ ] Every claim is labelled `VERIFIED`, `MEASURED`, `INTERPRETATION`,
  `HYPOTHESIS`, `FICTION`, `PLANNED`, or `UNKNOWN`.
- [ ] No founder cult, prophetic credential, guaranteed cure, guaranteed social
  outcome, or “works everywhere” statement appears in project material.
- [ ] Images, music, books, data and software have documented rights/provenance.
- [ ] Public corrections remain visible and link to the original claim.
- [ ] Beneficiary stories use specific, revocable consent and avoid exploitation.

## 6. Incident, correction and appeal path

1. **Make safe:** stop the affected pilot or data flow; protect people first.
2. **Preserve evidence:** timestamp logs, versions, decisions and witness reports.
3. **Notify:** safety, privacy, community and legal owners according to scope.
4. **Support affected people:** provide a human contact and practical remedy.
5. **Investigate independently:** include conflicts, incentives and governance,
   not only the immediate technical fault.
6. **Correct:** fix, compensate where appropriate, retrain, narrow, or close.
7. **Publish proportionately:** facts, uncertainty, impact, action and open items.
8. **Appeal:** an affected person can seek review by someone outside the original
   decision chain without retaliation.
9. **Reopen only through the full gate:** a patch alone does not authorize restart.

Israel's Privacy Protection Authority maintains a process for reporting serious
data-security incidents. [Official incident-reporting service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/report-of-data-breach)

## 7. Formation evidence packet

Before any filing, prepare one folder containing:

- charter, proposed purposes and entity-choice memorandum;
- founder/director identity and conflict disclosures;
- draft bylaws, delegated-authority matrix and signature policy;
- first-year plan, conservative budget and funding-source statement;
- commercial/charity/IP/data separation agreements;
- privacy inventory and data-protection impact assessment;
- insurance and regulated-activity map;
- lawyer's written review and accountant's written review;
- board decision approving the exact filing version.

The Corporations Authority's current proper-management guidance should be
reviewed against the final bylaws and operating procedures, not treated as a
box-checking certificate. [Official proper-management guidance](https://www.gov.il/he/BlobFolder/policy/associations-guidelines/he/associations-guidelines.pdf)
