# Safety and experiment gates

- Use low pressure and a mechanical relief valve independent of software.
- The acoustic chamber is equipment-only; never place a person or animal inside.
- Provide ventilation for the pump and electronics through acoustic baffles.
- Include physical emergency stop, current limiting and actuator end stops.
- Bound trial duration and impose cooldown periods for membranes and motors.
- Measure SPL and stop automatically above the approved threshold.
- Never allow a learned policy direct unbounded actuator or pump control.
- Begin with silent sensor calibration, then low-level air tests, then short tone
  trials behind a closed chamber.
- Human voice imitation and intelligible speech are later stages; first acceptance
  is a reproducible physical `/a/` with complete telemetry.
