### 2. The structure of the dataset is the main finding

- **Near-duplicates.** Same session + same arm + mel-shape correlation > 0.995: station 112 rows → **53 distinct sounds**; session 1: 56 rows → 38 sounds; session 2: 49 rows → **11 sounds** (`ah-free-1` ×16 all "good", `ma-nasal-0` ×8 and `ma-nasal-1` ×7 all "no"). Whole 1/2/3 set: 123 rows → **64 distinct sounds: 10 good, 17 close, 37 no.** Any pattern rests on **ten positives**.
- **Self-consistency.** Same-sound pairs inside a session: 230; identical verdict in **222 (97 %)**. His ear is stable; the label is not noisy.
- **Unit ↔ session confound (why the earlier judge "learned the session").** Station-1: ah 1 good / 6 no, ma 12 good / 2 no, ba 2 / 13. Station-2: ah 18 / 0 (one sound), ma 0 / 24, ba 0 / 3. The two sessions map unit→verdict in opposite directions, so anything trained on one session inverts on the other. Reproduced: all-feature logistic, ordinary 5-fold AUC **0.829**; leave-one-session-out pooled AUC **0.255** (inverted); the unit name alone, no audio, AUC 0.81 on rows. Leave-one-session-out for good-vs-no is therefore structurally uninformative on this data; leave-one-**arm**-out (arm = the generator run = the recording session of the sound) is the honest grouped test, and it is reported below.

### 5. His own words, tested

- **"The metric's favourites are noise."** Five clips carry the word רעש (3 station notes + blind3 B and C). Fixed HNR: noise clips median −1.1 dB, other "no" −1.7 dB, AUC noise-vs-other-no **0.53** (MW p = 0.41); flatness 0.52; voiced_frac 0.51. **HNR does not separate the clips he calls noise from the clips he merely rejects.** Separately, the metric's ten lowest-wdist clips all sit at HNR −1.6…−3.3 dB with voiced fraction 0.45, below the "good" median (+1.3 dB, 0.73) — the metric does prefer less periodic sounds, consistent with his complaint, but that is a statement about wdist, not a predictor of his ear.
- **"Child" / "adult" (blind, 05:29).** child = `ba-cv-10`: f0 223 Hz (68th pct of all clips), F3 2481; adult = `ma-nasal-103`: f0 107 Hz (21st pct), F3 2201 (13th pct), HNR +0.6; noise = `ba-cv-14`: pyin finds **no voiced frame** (voiced 0.00), HNR −2.5. f0 and F3 both point the way his words point — a lower pitch and a lower third formant read as an adult — and the "noise" clip is the one with no detectable periodicity. **n = 1 each: consistent, not evidence.** Across the 24-clip sitting the same direction appears (lower f0 → higher score, ρ = −0.45, p = 0.055) but 24 tests were run on 24 clips.
- **Human anchors (sitting 2026-09-01, 5 = "sounds human").** `ba_t00_0/1/2, ba_t01_0` — real recordings — scored **2, 3, 3, 3**; ERAN clips mean 2.5; Mann–Whitney p = 0.59. Either those references are bad audio (he said so on 08-30) or the 1–5 scale saturates at "something". Either way it is a **ceiling**: no synthetic clip can be expected to score above what his own recordings score.

### 6. What wdist actually tracks (station rows, Spearman)

duration −0.59, active duration −0.58, RMS spread −0.52, f0 +0.35, centroid +0.37. The metric rewards longer, flatter-level, lower-pitched, darker clips. His verdict correlates with none of those (duration ρ = −0.01 on sounds).

### 7. Verdict

**NEGATIVE, and clean.** With the data as it stands — 64 distinct sounds, 10 of them "good", units confounded with sessions, near-identical repeats inflating n by 2× — no simple interpretable acoustic feature, and no 3-feature logistic, predicts Iddo's 1/2/3 above chance under leave-one-arm-out (best 0.69, perm p 0.3). wdist stays at 0.48–0.54. The two prior negative results are confirmed and explained: the 0.95→0.49 judge learned the unit↔session map, which this dataset makes unavoidable.

The positive findings are about the **judge, not the audio**: he is 97 % self-consistent; his verdict is not a function of duration, level, formants or pitch; and his own human references do not pass his own 1–5 scale.

**Actionable (for whoever runs the next sitting):** (1) play distinct sounds, never repeats — a repeat costs a verdict and buys nothing; (2) mix every unit into every sitting so unit and session decorrelate; (3) put one clean human anchor per unit in every sitting to measure the ceiling; (4) collect at least ~40 distinct "good" sounds before training any judge on his ear.
