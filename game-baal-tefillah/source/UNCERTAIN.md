### 1. The Exact Ending Mechanism for the Land of Riches
**STATUS: SUBSTANTIALLY VERIFIED (2026-09-02)**

**MECHANISM CONFIRMED:**
The cure operates through a multi-stage transformation:
1. The Warrior carries a special Sword (two sharp edges, three powers unspecified)
2. A mystical path leads to a fiery mountain where a Lion dwells
3. Adjacent to this is a magical kitchen with foods cooked via channels from the mountain
4. The Warrior leads the wealth-obsessed people "against the wind" so the food's aroma reaches them
5. Consumption of the food causes their craving for money to dissipate entirely
6. Money becomes repulsive to them (compared to "excrement and filth")
7. They voluntarily cast away riches and bury themselves in shame
8. The Prayer Master then reforms all the corrupted lands

**Sources Verified:**
✓ Hebrew Wikisource: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/ספר_סיפורי_מעשיות/מבעל_תפילה
✓ English Translation Wikisource: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Translation:Tales_of_Rabbi_Nachman/12
✓ Multiple Breslov educational sites confirm these details

**What IS now verified:**
✓ The Sword has two sharp edges (חרב בעלת שתי קצוות)
✓ The Sword has three powers/forces (שלש כחות) - at least: "commanders flee when raised"
✓ There is a fiery mountain (הר של אש) with a Lion
✓ There is a magical kitchen (קעך) with food cooked via mountain channels
✓ Birds control fire intensity (in some versions)
✓ The cure is purely through food/sustenance transformation
✓ People's attitudes change from obsession to disgust/renunciation

**What REMAINS uncertain:**
⚠ The exact specification of the "three powers" - unclear whether:
  - Three separate points/edges on the blade?
  - Three distinct magical effects?
  - Three sequential applications?
  - Three revelatory truths?
⚠ The Lion's specific role - mentioned but function unclear
⚠ Water/river involvement - NO EXPLICIT MENTION FOUND in abstracts:

  - Does the Master of Prayer's sanctuary river play a role?
  - Is this purely aroma/taste, with no water element?
⚠ The "birds controlling fire" - confirmed in some versions but not all
⚠ The metaphysical nature: is this fairy-tale magic or spiritual metaphor or both?
