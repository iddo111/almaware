# The Groups and Their False Gods
## From Rabbi Nachman's Master of Prayer (Ba'al Tefillah)

Each group in the tale has chosen one false purpose as their ultimate god and organized their entire society around this worship.

---

## 1. THE LAND OF RICHES (LAND OF WEALTH) — מדינת העשירות
### The Most Fully Developed Group

**What They Worship:** Money itself; wealth and rich people as gods/deities

### Social Organization & Hierarchy
The society is structured entirely on a wealth-based hierarchy:

#### Classification System by Accumulated Wealth
People are ranked and designated by how much money they possess:
- **Poor/Common people:** Classified as "humans" (lowest status)
- **Wealthier:** Classified as "animals" 
- **Richer still:** Classified as "planetary beings" (stars, constellations)
- **Even wealthier:** Classified as "angels"
- **The very richest:** Classified as "gods"
- **The richest of all:** Classified as "gods of gods" (highest designation)

**VERIFIED:** This hierarchical system appears consistently across sources.

#### Physical Structure
- Fortified mountain cities
- Inaccessible to outsiders
- Protected by guards/defenders who question all who approach

#### Economic Practices
- **Strict prohibition on charity** — charity is forbidden because it diminishes accumulated wealth
- **International traders** — sent out to accumulate more money from other lands
- **Wealth concentration:** Money stays within the wealthy, never dispersed
- **Worship of wealth:** Portraits of rich people are displayed and worshipped through kissing and embracing them

#### Religious/Spiritual Practices
- **Human sacrifice to gods:** People offer themselves as sacrifices to wealthy "gods"
- **Belief in reincarnation through wealth:** They believe that through self-sacrifice (or service to the wealthy), one can be reborn/reincarnated into a wealthier status
- **Idolatry:** Wealthy people are treated as actual deities with divine protective power
- **Murder and robbery:** Despite belief in protective gods, murder and robbery are rampant, revealing the hollowness of their faith

#### Society's Fundamental Error
The Master of Prayer argues: "Money is not a purpose at all, and the main purpose is only serving Hashem [God]"

They reject this message entirely because they are "already so lost in their mistake that they already could not listen to anybody."

### How the Master of Prayer Approaches Them
- **Initial method:** Sends disciples to convince them that money is not the true purpose
- **Result:** Completely rejected
- **Personal infiltration:** The Prayer Meister disguises himself in various forms to enter their fortified cities
- **Challenge:** He debates their defenders about authentic spiritual purpose
- **Arrest:** He is captured and imprisoned in the Land of Riches for his teachings
- **Cosmic proof:** He references the mystical **Hand of the King** — a cosmic map containing all worlds, which proves their nation faces destruction if built on false gods

## 2. THE LAND OF HONOR
### What They Worship
Honor, prestige, and social status

### Society Organization
- **King/Leader:** An old, blind beggar whom they have elevated to kingship
- **Social value:** Determined entirely by position and honor
- **Purpose:** All activity directed toward gaining honor and respect

### Master of Prayer's Approach
He challenges their belief that honor is the ultimate purpose.

---

## 3. THE LAND OF MURDER (DESTRUCTION)
### What They Worship
Destruction and annihilation as the ultimate goal

### Philosophical Position
- "All things on earth must ultimately cease to exist"
- "Destruction is the very goal of all things"
- They elevate destruction as sacred purpose

### Society Organization
- **King/Leader:** A person who committed patricide (killed their own father) — showing commitment to destruction
- Worship of death and ending

### Master of Prayer's Approach
He teaches that destruction is not the ultimate purpose; creation and divine service are.

---

## 4. THE LAND OF FOOD / THE LAND OF THE BODY
### What They Worship
Physical abundance, consumption, and large bodies as signs of divine favor

### Society Organization
- **King/Leader:** A tall, well-fed man
- **Value system:** Physical size, strength, and abundance of food
- **Culture:** Devoted to eating, feasting, and physical gratification

### Master of Prayer's Approach
He teaches that physical sustenance is not the ultimate purpose of existence.

---

## 5. THE LAND OF BEAUTY
### What They Worship
Physical beauty and attractiveness; viewed as divine

### Society Organization
- **Queen/Leader:** A beautiful woman whom they have elevated to leadership
- **Purpose:** Population growth and reproduction through beauty
- **Value:** Physical attractiveness as the measure of worth

### Master of Prayer's Approach
He teaches that beauty is not the ultimate purpose; spiritual beauty and divine service are.

---

## 6. THE LAND OF SPEECH / THE LAND OF ELOQUENCE
### What They Worship
Language, eloquence, and the power of words

### Society Organization
- **Leader:** A multilingual lunatic/fool whose speech is celebrated
- **Value:** The ability to speak many languages and use eloquent language
- **Culture:** Speech and rhetoric elevated as humanity's highest achievement

### Master of Prayer's Approach
He teaches that speech and language, while powerful, are not the ultimate purpose.

---

## 7. THE LAND OF WISDOM
### What They Worship
Knowledge and intellectual understanding as the highest good

### Society Organization
- **Leader:** A great sage/wise man
- **Value:** Knowledge, learning, and intellectual achievement
- **Culture:** Devoted to study and philosophical inquiry

### Master of Prayer's Approach
**VERIFIED NOTE:** Even the faction "that had chosen that the main thing is wisdom has not reached the true purpose and also need to be led out from that" — meaning wisdom without divine service is still false purpose.

---

## 8. THE LAND OF INTOXICATION / THE LAND OF DRUNKENNESS
### What They Worship
Constant intoxication and escapism through drunkenness

### Society Organization
- **King/Leader:** An inebriated man constantly drunk
- **Purpose:** Achieving and maintaining perpetual drunkenness
- **Culture:** Escape from reality through intoxication

### Master of Prayer's Approach
He teaches that escapism is not the true purpose; clarity and divine consciousness are.

---

## 9. THE TRUE GROUP — THE PRAYER COMMUNITY
### What They Worship
The One True God through prayer and devotion

### Society Organization
- **King/Leader:** The Master of Prayer (Ba'al Tefillah) himself
- **Purpose:** Prayer, praise, confession, repentance, fasting, self-mortification, and service to God
- **Practice:** Constant prayer, songs of praise, heartfelt confession, voluntary self-discipline
- **Location:** Outside civilization, with a river flowing and fruit trees — removed from worldly distractions

### Characteristics
This is the **only true purpose** according to the tale. All other groups are fallen or misdirected parts of the royal court.

---

## Key Patterns Across All Groups

**Common Structure:**
1. Each group has elevated one specific value or practice to the status of ultimate god
2. Each has chosen a leader embodying that false god (or elevating a person as its representative)
3. Each has organized their entire society around this false worship
4. Each rejects the Master of Prayer's message that their chosen purpose is hollow

**Common Error:**
Taking what may be a legitimate human activity (eating, speaking, acquiring knowledge, seeking honor) and elevating it to THE ultimate purpose, as if these material or intellectual pursuits were divine.

**Common Redemption:**
Each group is redeemed only when:
- They are reconnected with the true court
- They recognize the false nature of their worship
- They are redirected toward authentic divine service through prayer

## For Game Design: Key Gameplay Elements

The groups offer rich material for gameplay:
1. **Moral/philosophical contrast:** Each represents a common human obsession rendered absurd
2. **Distinct culture:** Each would have unique visual aesthetic, dialogue, values, economics
3. **Spiritual progression:** Player journey involves recognizing the falseness of each and redirecting groups toward truth
4. **Redemption mechanics:** Possibility of converting/healing each group through spiritual intervention
5. **Hierarchy systems:** Land of Riches in particular offers complex simulation potential with wealth-based ranks
