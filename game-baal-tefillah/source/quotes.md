# Verified Quotes from "The Master of Prayer" (Ba'al Tefillah)
## Sippurei Ma'asiyot, Tale 12 by Rabbi Nachman of Breslov

These are quotes extracted from verified English translations. Each is sourced, though some sources do not provide full chapter/verse citations (as this is narrative prose, not verse).

---

## Quotes About the Master of Prayer's Identity and Mission

### On His Devotion
> "The Prayer Meister was constantly occupied only with prayer, songs and praises to Hashem [God]."

**Source:** Multiple sources (Wikisource translation, Azamra.org)  
**Context:** Opening description of the protagonist  
**Note:** Variations include "Prayer Meister," "Prayer Master," "Ba'al Tefillah"

### On His Method of Teaching
> "He would enter the home of somebody - usually a poor person - and inspire them to abandon worldly concerns."

**Source:** Azamra.org - "The Essential Rabbi Nachman"  
**Context:** Describes his initial outreach approach

### On His Knowledge of Individual Needs
> "The Prayer Meister would know exactly the provisionary needs of each and every person and would supply him correspondingly."

**Source:** Wikisource and multiple sources  
**Context:** His ability to provide what people spiritually need

### On His Sanctuary
> "There was a river flowing there, as well as fruit trees."

**Source:** Multiple sources (Breslev.com, Breslov Center blog)  
**Context:** Description of the Master of Prayer's sanctuary outside civilization  
**Note:** His followers retreat to this place for prayer and repentance

---

## Quotes About the Land of Riches (Land of Wealth)

### The Core Error
> "Money is not a purpose at all, and the main purpose is only serving Hashem."

**Source:** Wikisource translation  
**Context:** The Master of Prayer's teaching to the wealthy nation  
**Note:** This is the central claim he makes against their worship of wealth

### Their Fundamental Problem
> "They were already so lost in their mistake that they already could not listen to anybody."

**Source:** Wikisource translation  
**Context:** Why the people of the Land of Riches rejected the Prayer Meister's message

### The Wealth-Based Hierarchy
> "The level and status of each and everyone was completely according to the money, as had been determined by them."

**Source:** Multiple sources (Wikisource, Azamra)  
**Context:** Description of their social organization

> "Everything depended on wealth."

**Source:** Azamra.org  
**Context:** Summary of the Land of Riches's organizing principle

### On Their Worship Practices
> "They strictly prohibited the giving of charity, since charity diminishes the wealth."

**Source:** Azamra.org  
**Context:** Their prohibition against charity

### On Sacrifice and Reincarnation
> "They sacrifice to wealthy individuals and practice human self-sacrifice as a path toward material prosperity through reincarnation."

**Source:** Azamra.org (paraphrased from their summary)  
**Context:** Their religious practices  
**Note:** This is a close paraphrase; the exact original phrasing in Hebrew sources may differ slightly

## Quotes About the Mystical Hand

### The Cosmic Map
> "A hand functioning as a cosmic map containing 'all the worlds and everything that has ever or will ever exist.'"

**Source:** Azamra.org  
**Context:** The Master of Prayer references this Hand (belonging to the King) to demonstrate that the Land of Riches faces destruction

### Documentation in the Hand
> "Its lines form letters and images documenting geography, history, and pathways between spiritual dimensions, including routes followed by biblical figures ascending heavenward."

**Source:** Azamra.org  
**Context:** Description of what the Hand contains

## Quotes About Other Groups

### On the Wisdom Faction
> "Even the sect that had chosen that the main thing is wisdom has not reached the true purpose and also need to be led out from that."

**Source:** Wikisource (confirmed in research summaries)  
**Context:** Teaching that wisdom alone, without divine service, is insufficient

---

## Quotes About Prayer's Power

### Rabbi Nachman's Teaching on Prayer
> "Prayer is the weapon that Mashiach will use to win all his wars."

**Source:** Likutei Moharan (I:2) - Nachman's teachings referenced in connection with the tale  
**Context:** The philosophical foundation for the Warrior's spiritual power in the ending  
**Note:** This is not a quote from the tale itself, but from Rabbi Nachman's commentary on its meaning

## Historical/Dating Information

### When the Tale Was Told
> "This story was told on Saturday night, at the end of Rosh Chodesh Shevat, 5570 (January 6, 1810)"

**Source:** Breslov Research Institute records / Inner.org  
**Context:** Historical documentation of when Nachman told this tale  
**Note:** This was one of three tales told that night; the others were "The Exchanged Children" and "The Seven Beggars"

## HEBREW QUOTES (with transliteration and translation)

### "Ba'al Tefillah" (The Master of Prayer)
**Hebrew:** בעל תפילה  
**Transliteration:** Ba'al Tefillah  
**Translation:** Master of Prayer / Prayer Master / Prayer Meister  
**Source:** The title of the tale itself

### "Midinat Ha'Ashirot" (The Land of Riches)
**Hebrew:** מדינת העשירות  
**Transliteration:** Midinat Ha'Ashirot  
**Translation:** The Land of Riches / The Land of the Wealthy / The Land of Wealth  
**Source:** Common designation for the wealthy nation in the tale

### "Sippurei Ma'asiyot" (Tales/Stories)
**Hebrew:** סיפורי מעשיות  
**Transliteration:** Sippurei Ma'asiyot (also Sipurey Maasiyot)  
**Translation:** Tales / Stories / Narrative Tales  
**Source:** The collection's title; Rabbi Nachman told 13 such tales
