# Tale Structure: The Master of Prayer (Ba'al Tefillah)
## 12th Tale of Sippurei Ma'asiyot by Rabbi Nachman of Breslov

### Narrative Sequence

#### 1. The Cosmic Storm Wind
A great storm wind comes upon the world, violently mixing and overturning everything:
- Sea becomes dry land
- Dry land becomes sea
- Wilderness becomes settled area
- Settled area becomes wilderness

The Storm Wind scatters the royal court, dispersing its members across different regions.

#### 2. The Scattered Court (Members)
The following court members are separated by the storm:
- **The King** — the sovereign ruler
- **The Queen** — the queen consort
- **The Queen's Daughter** (also called "The Princess") — the royal princess
- **A Child** — the child of the Queen's Daughter (or a young child in the court)
- **The Wise Man** (or "Sage") — advisor and scholar
- **The Bard/Orator** (Hebrew: The speaker or one skilled in speech)
- **The Treasurer** — keeper of royal treasury
- **The Faithful Friend** — devoted companion
- **The Mighty Warrior** (or "Strong Man") — military commander/hero who has conquered many lands
- **The Master of Prayer** (Ba'al Tefillah / Prayer Meister) — protagonist

**UNCERTAINTY:** The exact order of this list and precise Hebrew titles vary between sources. Some versions distinguish between "Prayer Leader" and other titles. The total count and names listed above represent the most consistent rendering across sources consulted.

#### 3. The Master of Prayer's Initial Solitude
The Master of Prayer lives apart from civilization in isolation. He is constantly occupied with:
- Prayer
- Songs and praises to God
- Communion with the Divine

#### 4. The Master of Prayer's Outreach
The Prayer Meister begins to venture into settled areas to draw people back to spiritual service. His methods:
- He would enter homes of common people (especially the poor)
- He would engage in heart-to-heart conversations
- He used various disguises and transfigurations—appearing differently to different people
- He would convince followers to abandon worldly pursuits and return with him
- He would provide for their spiritual needs exactly

Those who accepted would be taken to his isolated sanctuary featuring:
- A flowing river
- Fruit trees
- Space for prayer and repentance
- Community engaged in devotion

#### 5. Discovery of the Scattered Groups
The Master of Prayer discovers that the world has organized into distinct groups (factions/nations), each having adopted a false god or false purpose:

These groups include (detailed separately in **the-groups.md**):
- Land of Riches/Wealth (מדינת העשירות)
- Land of Honor
- Land of Murder/Destruction
- Land of Food/Body
- Land of Beauty
- Land of Speech/Eloquence
- Land of Wisdom
- Land of Intoxication/Drunkenness
- The True Prayer Community (the one correct group)

#### 6. The Master of Prayer's Encounters
The Prayer Meister engages with each group:
- He attempts to convince them of the falseness of their worship
- He is initially rejected by most
- He is arrested and imprisoned in the Land of Riches
- He uses spiritual arguments and revelation of higher truths
- He references a mystical **Hand** (belonging to the King) that contains cosmic knowledge and maps all worlds

#### 7. Reunion of the Court
The Master of Prayer gradually discovers and encounters the separated members of the court:
- Each has become a leader/ruler of one of the false factions
- Each represents a different land or group
- Recognition and reunion begin to occur

#### 8. The Restoration and Healing
Once the full court is reunited, they gain collective authority to reform all the nations. Key elements:
- The **Mighty Warrior** is given a special role
- He possesses a mystical **Sword** with particular properties:
  - Three powers/paths
  - Two sharp edges
  - Described in connection with a fiery mountain and a magical kitchen
- **UNCERTAINTY:** The exact mechanism by which water/spring/river enters the cure is not fully documented in available sources
- The **Land of Riches** is specifically healed/cured through the Warrior's intervention
- All groups are redirected toward authentic spiritual purpose

#### 9. Resolution
The tale concludes with the spiritual correction of humanity and the triumph of genuine divine service over false gods and material values.
