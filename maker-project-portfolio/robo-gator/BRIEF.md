# Robo-Gator Concept

## Goal

Create a friendly, visibly robotic alligator for tabletop or dry-land display: articulated tail/body motion, LEDs, sound, and slow supervised locomotion.

## Safety boundary

No jaw-closing mechanism, teeth, projectile, camera-surveillance deployment, animal interaction, unsupervised roaming, or water operation in POC-1. Use rounded soft exterior features, guarded drives, low speed, physical kill switch, and a tethered test area.

## First POC

One stationary tail segment with a limited mechanical stop and low-voltage actuator. Demonstrate repeatable motion without heat, pinch access, or loose parts.
