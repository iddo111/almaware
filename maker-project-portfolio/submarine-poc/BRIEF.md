# K2 Plus Internal-Propulsor Submarine POC

## Goal

Create the largest **practical, uncrewed, tether-retrievable** modular POC from a K2 Plus—not a pressure vessel and not a human-carrying craft. Creality lists a 350 × 350 × 350 mm build volume, so the first hull module must fit with margin for warping and sealing features. [Creality K2 Plus specifications](https://store.creality.com/ca/products/creality-k2-plus-combo-3d-printer)

## Architecture

- Segmented, smooth external fairing around a separate low-depth wet duct.
- Internal spiral impeller demonstration module, mechanically isolated from flotation/ballast volumes.
- Ballast demonstration uses non-pressurized test tanks only; compressed-air systems are excluded from POC-1.
- Tether, positive buoyancy, manual recovery, and shallow controlled-water testing only.

## Material policy

- Candidate wet parts: PETG or ASA only after printed coupon and soak tests.
- PLA: visual prototypes only.
- Carbon-fibre filled material: fixtures only; not pressure/watertight surfaces.
- Recycled-bottle filament: visual or noncritical prototypes only until characterized.

## POC-1 acceptance

Dry fit; measured rotor clearance; isolated buoyancy volume; static leak test in a container; positive recovery buoyancy; no autonomous operation.
