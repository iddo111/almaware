# Urban Vehicle Ecosystem — Research Frame

## Question

How should people, cargo, micromobility, transit, accessibility devices, energy, parking, repair, and street space work as one urban system rather than as competing vehicle products?

## Research lanes

- Accessible first/last kilometre and mobility-aid integration.
- Cargo consolidation, neighbourhood logistics, and repairability.
- Transit interchange, safe walking/cycling, and curb allocation.
- Shared charging, solar/storage, and lifecycle material recovery.
- Governance: privacy, safety, insurance, public space, pricing, and failure response.

## First artifact

Create three fictional neighbourhood scenarios—dense centre, suburban edge, and low-service area—and score each against travel time, accessibility, energy, cost, safety, and public-space use. No vehicle deployment is implied.

## Devil's advocate

An “ecosystem” can become surveillance, exclusion, vendor lock-in, or a gadget collection. Every concept must show who benefits, who is burdened, what happens without a phone/account, and what remains public infrastructure.
