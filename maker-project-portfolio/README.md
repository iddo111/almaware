## Current triage priority

The primary robotics track is `k2-modular-android-body`. Its baseline is now a
modular exoskeleton developed in stages: waist-to-below-knee, full lower body,
full body, then an independently operated android platform. Human-worn work is
not authorized until the non-human test and safety gates in its brief pass.
The folding flight-suit/air-taxi extension is active research only; powered
lift and human flight remain blocked behind a separate aviation-grade program.
