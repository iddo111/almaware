# Locust-Gaiter Robot

## Intent

Explore a robot that shares a body platform between a gaiter/gator-inspired walking form and a locust-inspired jumping/flying form, using telescopic leg research as a common mechanism.

## POC order

1. Static K2 Plus-printed body module and one passive telescopic-leg sample.
2. Tethered, low-energy single-leg motion on a bench.
3. Controlled jump-energy measurement into a fully enclosed test box.
4. Only after repeatable safe landing: assess a single non-propulsive gliding/falling experiment.

## Hard exclusions

No free-flight vehicle, no outdoor operation, no camera/surveillance payload, no autonomous navigation, no swarm, no thousands of units, and no animal interaction. A swarm is a separate systems/safety programme, not the next prototype.

## Devil's advocate

Telescopic legs add friction, bending failure, cable routing, and pinch hazards. Jumping and flight impose very different structural and energy demands; a shared shape is plausible, but a shared actuator/train may be the wrong compromise. Prove one leg before designing the insect.
