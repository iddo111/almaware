# Stage 0A — Cardboard Hip-to-Below-Knee Mock-up

Status: `AUTHORIZED INERT FIT MOCK-UP / NO LOAD / NO POWER`

## Purpose

Validate dimensions, joint-axis placement, cuff clearance, sitting geometry and
basic stair-pose range before CAD, printing, metal structure or motors.

This model must not support body weight, apply assistance, be worn while walking
or be tested on stairs. Use it on a table/dummy first, then only hold it loosely
against the body for a static fit check with a helper.

## Materials

- Double-wall corrugated cardboard.
- Hot-glue gun and low-temperature glue sticks where available.
- Metal ruler, marker, craft knife and cutting mat.
- Four M5 x 25–35 mm bolts, wide washers and wing nuts, or strong split-pin
  paper fasteners for the first articulation test.
- Hook-and-loop straps or masking tape for temporary cuffs.
- Drinking-straw or rolled-cardboard spacers.
- String for body circumferences.

## Measurements

- `A`: external hip reference `H` to knee axis `K`, left and right.
- `B`: knee axis `K` to proposed lower-cuff centre, left and right.
- `C`: belt centre to hip reference `H`, left and right.
- `D`: thigh circumference 150 mm above `K`, left and right.
- `E`: calf circumference at the lower-cuff centre, left and right.
- Waist-belt circumference and outside hip-to-hip width.

Record all values in millimetres. Do not average left and right.

## Cardboard parts for one leg

1. Pelvis side plate: 180 x 120 mm, trimmed after fitting.
2. Thigh rail: length `A`, width 55 mm.
3. Lower rail: length `B + 80 mm`, width 50 mm.
4. Two hip hinge discs: 90 mm diameter.
5. Two knee hinge discs: 80 mm diameter.
6. Thigh cuff band: body-contact length approximately `0.55 x D`, 70 mm wide.
7. Calf cuff band: body-contact length approximately `0.55 x E`, 65 mm wide.
8. Hard-stop tabs and replaceable cardboard spacers.

Make every rail and side plate from three laminated layers. Rotate the middle
layer so its corrugation crosses the outer layers. Hot-glue the layers on a flat
surface, weight them until cool, and leave the hinge regions free of glue lumps.

## Assembly

1. Mark `H` and `K` on a full-height paper or cardboard body outline.
2. Laminate the pelvis plate, thigh rail and lower rail.
3. Drill/punch the hip pivot through the pelvis plate and thigh rail at `H`.
4. Drill/punch the knee pivot through the thigh and lower rails at `K`.
5. Use bolts plus large washers; tighten only enough to remove side play while
   preserving free rotation.
6. Glue cuff spacers to the rails, but attach cuff bands with removable tape or
   hook-and-loop—not permanent glue.
7. Add replaceable hard-stop tabs only after confirming the mock-up does not
   bind through standing, sitting and an unweighted stair pose on the outline.

## Fit checks

- Hip and knee pivot marks remain aligned in neutral and simulated flexion.
- Cuffs avoid the groin crease, kneecap, back of knee, tibial tuberosity and
  fibular head.
- Rails do not contact each other or the body outline through the required arc.
- Model can be removed in under five seconds without tools.
- No part is treated as load-bearing.

Photograph front, side and rear views with the ruler visible. Record any axis
migration, binding, cardboard crushing or cuff interference before changing the
dimensions.

## Stop conditions

Stop after any hot-glue burn, sharp exposed fastener, pinching, skin pressure,
axis migration or structural buckling. Never strengthen a failed cardboard
model and then use it for loading; redesign the geometry instead.
