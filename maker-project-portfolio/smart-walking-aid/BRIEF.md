# Smart Walking-Aid Concept

## Goal

Build a **non-load-bearing** clip-on sensing and feedback module for an existing walker: lighting, button, battery indication, optional caregiver alert simulation, and data logging.

## Explicit exclusions

No fall prevention claim, braking, powered steering, body-weight support, medical diagnosis, medication guidance, emergency dispatch, or unsupervised user trial.

## First POC

Bench-mounted enclosure and mock handle. Verify readable status, deliberate emergency-stop behavior, safe battery enclosure, no pinch points, and failure-to-off. Any future human test needs a consent plan and qualified clinical/safety review.
