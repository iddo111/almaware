# Iddo Perez Carbon Guitar Pick

## Status

Low-priority active background project. No purchase, machining, or print has been authorized yet.

## Product intent

- Compact pointed pick inspired by the handling envelope of Jazz III and Attak Pik products, while using an original outline and surface treatment.
- Firm, repeatable grip.
- Sharp, accurate playing tip with controlled edge bevel.
- Highest practical strength and durability for the selected manufacturing process.
- Branding: `עידו פרץ` and `iddoperez.ai`.
- Prototype palette: black filament only. Grip texture and branding must be produced geometrically, using relief and surface-finish contrast rather than additional colors.

## Approved visual direction

- Concept V1 approved by Iddo.
- Black carbon-style body, compact pointed outline, polished-looking edge, and central micro-hex grip.
- Translate the silver lettering and green accent seen in the visualization into same-color embossed/debossed geometry for the physical black-only prototype.

## Design lanes

1. Carbon-fiber-filled engineering polymer prototype for fast geometry iteration.
2. Solid carbon-fiber laminate production candidate, CNC cut and fully edge-sealed.
3. Non-carbon control samples for blind comfort, grip, wear, and tone comparison.

## Safety and truth gates

- Carbon-filled filament is not the same material as continuous carbon laminate.
- No exposed fibers, sharp splinters, or unsealed machined edges may contact the player.
- Carbon dust machining requires containment, extraction, eye/respiratory protection, and suitable tooling.
- Final claims require physical measurements and blind play testing; CAD or slicer output alone is not acceptance.

## Initial acceptance targets

- Dimensions and tip geometry documented and reproducible.
- Dry and damp grip tests with no uncontrolled slipping.
- No edge delamination, splintering, or exposed fibers after wear testing.
- Branding remains legible without weakening or irritating the grip surface.
- At least three thickness/tip variants are compared before locking the production geometry.
