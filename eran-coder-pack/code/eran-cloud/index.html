This is the working notebook of a small, self-funded lab building Eran: a voice-first AI child that learns the way an infant does — from raw sound before letters, on hardware we own, with a training record anyone can check. Around it sits the rest of the bench: an evolutionary colony of tiny on-device voice models, a four-node edge cluster, a from-kernel edge OS, and a hardware-neuron lab that publishes its failures.

Nothing here is a product. Every entry below carries a status label, and the labels are the point.

The naming rule

The language model that exists today is called ProtoEran. The name Eran is reserved for the full, realised, embodied child — a thing that does not exist. Renaming it downward was a deliberate act: the honest name is the smaller one.

Track 1 — Eran ער"ן · הילד

The core project. An AI raised from zero, voice-first, on a corpus of one person's own speech, on hardware we own. The Hebrew name ער"ן is itself an acronym — רשת נוירולוגית עצבית, "neural network." The beloved name and the built thing are the same word. Full page →

ProtoEran Prototype

A from-scratch decoder-only language model, 3.23M parameters, byte-level vocabulary of 260 — no tokenizer, no pretrained weights, random init at "age zero".

  • Trained only on one speaker's own dictation — a hard guard in the data loader refuses any book corpus by path.
  • Bytes, not words, so Hebrew and English share one alphabet and letter-level similarity survives.
  • Honest ceiling: it babbles that speaker's phrasing. It is not fluent and does not converse.

last checkpoint 2026-07-11 · step 625,454 · loss 0.375

Vocal cortex Prototype

The voice-first line — a model over neural audio codes, no text anywhere in the loop, in the spirit of textless NLP.

Three named faculties, in birth order of their namesakes: language, thought, perception.

HoloBrain Concept

The architectural north-star: a world-model, a concept model and an embodied loop meeting in one shared gist space — predict the essence of what comes next, not the raw surface.

  • Step one is concrete and buildable: a JEPA-style objective that predicts a masked span's embedding rather than its bytes.
  • Honest: unifying language models, world models and robotics is an open research frontier. We are not claiming to have solved it.

The autonomy law Live

The point of the colony is what a model learns by itself. So the rules are strict, and they cost us performance on purpose:

  • Never hand-curated or hand-labelled batches. No vowel-weighted sampling.
  • Allowed: evolving the organism (better ears as a new kind of ant), curiosity dynamics, colony selection pressure, uncurated environment streams.
  • Target: reach word level emergently, composed from syllables the colony already mastered — never taught.

a spoon-fed colony would prove nothing

A trap worth publishing

Corpus manifests stored a hash computed after float conversion, so it could never match the hash of the raw source file. Freshness had to be decided by session date, not by content hash. Cost: one aborted training run. It is written down here so it costs someone else nothing.

Connectome models Prototype

Built the right way round: real published wiring in, known biological dynamics out.

  • C. elegans tap-withdrawal reflex from the published connectome — head tap reverses, tail tap advances, both correct.
  • Fly central-complex heading circuit from 172 real neurons and 8,605 synapses — the bump holds heading and tracks a cue to about 13°, and the ring topology falls out of the wiring rather than being imposed.

ALMA Language Concept

A visual, node-based language for building and training models with your hands — and, more seriously, a readable symbolic map layered over a neural substrate so a model's world-model can be inspected and edited.

  • Honest limit, stated up front: lossless symbolic compression of neural knowledge is unsolved. The target is a hybrid — a readable map above a neural base — not a full translation.

Lab notebook יומן מעבדה

Selected entries, including the ones that hurt. A notebook that records only successes is marketing.

  • 2026-06-18 ProtoEran is born A 3.23M-parameter byte-level model, random init, one morning: loss 5.64 → ~1.0. The sample log shows the arc — pure binary noise, then Hebrew words emerging, then the speaker's own phrasing. Honest: babble, not fluency.
  • 2026-06-22 200G fabric link confirmed between two nodes Port up at 200,000 Mb/s, peer discovered over link-local addressing, 3/3 pings, 0% loss, 0.9 ms — no manual addressing needed at all. Discovering that the earlier manual configuration had been unnecessary was itself the finding.
  • 2026-06-29 First sound The audio cortex ran to completion and produced the first machine-generated babble in this lab — from 222.8 minutes of one person's voice, with no text anywhere in the pipeline. It is not a clean vowel. It is a sound the model made.
  • 2026-06-29 Self-inflicted broadcast storm Bridging two fabric ports closed a hidden physical loop in the cabling; multicast receives climbed past 900 million packets and pegged a node. Root-caused on 2026-07-03 to a bridge defined in the network config and therefore recreated on every boot. Standing rule since: routed point-to-point links only, never bridge the fabric.
  • 2026-07-02 Architecture bake-off, both arms built Modular specialists scored 0.785 against 0.665 for one fused pass — but the decisive gap was structural, not accuracy: 13/13 scenarios survived a component fault versus 0/13. Conclusion adopted: specialists inside one brain, with a learned gate.
  • 2026-07-15 Negative result — the salt cell has no memristance Five automated set/reset cycles, sixty reads, every one of them 0 Ω. Zero separation. The cause was mechanical, not chemical: a soaked paper substrate cannot hold a sub-millimetre gap, so the electrodes touched. Published rather than retried quietly.
  • 2026-08-05 The fabric was never broken Rebuilt and measured at 109–110 Gbit/s per link with zero retransmits. Weeks of "dead fabric" turned out to be network profiles bound to interfaces with no cable in them — they could never activate. Diagnostic rule adopted: read carrier state, not ping.
  • 2026-08-11 Negative result — voice fine-tune fails on Hebrew Run 1 of a personal speech-recognition adapter came back worse on Hebrew than the base model: 37.1% word error rate against 30.7%. English improved. Diagnosis: a self-distillation trap — the adapter was learning the base model's own mistakes. Reported as a failure, not buried.
  • 2026-08-11 A new queen The colony crowned a new best ant on the fixed held-out protocol: top-1 cosine 0.758, up from 0.424. The baseline was reproduced bit-exact before the comparison was allowed to count, and a rollback path was written down before the crown changed hands.
  • 2026-08-12 Round 4: the queen holds Three challengers born from fresh seeds and a scaling change; all three fell short of significance. A tournament where the incumbent usually wins is a tournament with an honest judge.

What this lab does not claim מה איננו טוענים

Written down so no reader has to guess where the edge is.

About the models

  • ProtoEran does not converse, reason, or understand. It emits fragments in the style of the speech it was trained on.
  • The vocal model babbles. It cannot say a clean vowel on command, and it certainly cannot speak.
  • No model here is competitive with a frontier system, and none is trying to be. The whole design bets on grounding and continual learning, not scale.

About the hardware

  • The analog neuromorphic work is not quantum computing. There are no qubits and no entanglement, and it breaks no cryptography.
  • The frequency-multiplexing idea at the centre of that design has, as far as our own literature search found, no published support. It is untested speculation and is labelled as such.
  • A four-node edge cluster is a real cluster and a small one. Where a job needs more than it has, we say so instead of pretending.
