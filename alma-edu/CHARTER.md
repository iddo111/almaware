## 2. The thesis

Every school in the world teaches a child *about* the world. ALMIDO teaches a child *through* the world — the same way a scientist actually learns, and the same way we are teaching Eran:

**לימוד מעוגן חוויה — learning anchored in experience.** Attach the child to something that already has consequences, and the consequences become the teacher. No worksheets, no scores, no screens for the small ones.

The three engines, taken from biology and used honestly:

1. **Hunger, not feeding.** A child who is handed everything never asks. We set out a rich room and let curiosity choose. *(Same law as Eran's: appetite ranks the menu, nobody spoon-feeds.)*
2. **The body before the symbol.** A four-year-old's shoulder can draw an א a year before the fingers can. Movement first, then the mark. *(Same law: Eran hears before he speaks.)*
3. **The parent's face is the reward.** Not a sticker, not a chart, not a grade. Bonding is the strongest learning signal a human has, and it costs nothing. *(Same law: the parent's tap is what pays Eran's synapses.)*

## 4. The arc — letters are only the first rung

The alphabet is where ALMIDO starts because reading is the gate to everything else. But the method is the point, and it keeps going:

- **4–6 · אותיות** — the body becomes the letter; song carries the sequence; the child reads a pointed verse. *(built)*
- **6–7 · מספר ומידה** — counting with beads and a real abacus, then **measuring**: length, weight, time, temperature. The moment a child writes down a number they measured themselves, they have done science.
- **7–8 · לשאול שאלה** — the experiment as a form: a question, a guess written down *before*, a measurement, and an honest answer even when the guess was wrong. **Negative results are results** — taught at seven.
- **8–10 · לבנות** — the printer, the CNC, soldering, a motor that turns because you made it turn. E-waste in, robot out. *(This is the ERAN Negev centre, in miniature, in a room.)*
- **10+ · לגדל משהו חי** — worms, ants, a plant, a colony. Observe, measure, and be honest about what you saw. The oldest science there is, and Iddo is an agronomist — this rung is his.

## 5. The laws (non-negotiable)

1. **No screens for the small ones.** Feedback is sound, light, and objects in the hand.
2. **A child is never wrong about a letter.** The moment being wrong is possible, shame arrives, and shame is the thing this whole program exists to prevent.
3. **Every child gets a line on the beam.** Decided before anyone reaches it. A monument that records who made it is a monument to exclusion.
4. **Dose beats method.** Twenty minutes a day that a child volunteers for beats a brilliant hour they endure. Everything here exists to make the small daily dose wanted.
5. **Never fake a result** — not the child's, not ours. If a child cannot decode yet, that is the honest state, and we say it.
6. **The parent is inside, not outside.** ALMIDO is not a service that takes your child away for an hour. The adult is in the room, singing badly, and that is the mechanism.

## 6. The smallest real start

Not a school. Not an app. **Six children in a neighbourhood, one adult, forty minutes, three days a week, for eight weeks.** One lamp, one strip of tape, a bucket of wooden discs, and the song.

**What would honestly prove it worked:** at eight weeks, each child recognises their own name's first letter out of a spread of six, and asks — unprompted — for a letter that hasn't arrived yet. That second one is the real measurement, because it means the hunger engine caught.

**What would show it did not:** the children come because they are told to.

## 7. The honest ledger

- The two-year promise (reading a pointed verse at six) is what cheder does and has done for centuries — **with a daily dose no gan currently gives**. The dose is the risk, not the method.
- Nothing here has been run with a real child yet. Every number in this project is currently zero.
- The tracked wand is unproven with children and may turn out to be a distraction from a wooden tile. It arrives last for exactly that reason.
- ALMIDO and Eran share a curriculum on purpose, and that is a genuine experiment — but a four-year-old is not a control group and will never be treated as one.
