# Timeline: The Munich Massacre, 5-6 September 1972

Hour-by-hour reconstruction of the hostage crisis and rescue attempt, based on published sources.

## TUESDAY, 5 SEPTEMBER 1972

### 04:30 – Initial Assault

Eight Palestinian terrorists wearing Olympic tracksuits and carrying gym bags filled with grenades and automatic weapons breach the Olympic Village security in the early morning darkness. They approach the Israeli team's quarters.

- **Location:** Connollystraße 31, Building 31, Israeli Olympic Village dormitory
- **First alert:** Yossef Gutfreund (wrestling referee) hears noise at the door of the Israeli quarters around 4:30 AM, investigates, and sees masked gunmen with weapons
- **Heroic action:** Gutfreund throws his 6-foot-3-inch, 290-pound frame against the door and screams a warning to fellow athletes. The alert allows weightlifting coach Tuvia Sokolsky to smash a window and escape, surviving the attack.
- **Source:** [Wikipedia: Munich massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich_massacre); [CNN: A survivor's story](https://edition.cnn.com/2012/07/27/sport/olympics-2012-munich-shaul-ladany-survivor)

### 04:30–05:00 – First Killings

The terrorists storm into the Israeli quarters. Two athletes are killed immediately.

- **Moshe Weinberg** (wrestling coach, 32): Shot by terrorists and his body thrown into the street
- **Yossef Romano** (weightlifter, 32): Shot during the initial assault
- **Source:** [History.com: Munich Massacre](https://www.history.com/articles/munich-massacre-olympics); [Wikipedia: Moshe Weinberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moshe_Weinberg)

### 05:00–10:00 – Hostage-Taking and Negotiations Begin

The terrorists take nine remaining athletes and coaches hostage inside the dormitory. They barricade themselves in and begin making demands via telephone.

**Hostages held:**
- David Berger (weightlifter, 28)
- Ze'ev Friedman (weightlifter, 28)
- Eliezer Halfin (wrestler, 24)
- Mark Slavin (wrestler, 18)
- Amitzur Shapira (track and field coach, 40)
- Kehat Shorr (shooting coach, 53)
- Andre Spitzer (fencing master, 27)
- Yakov Springer (weightlifting judge, 51)
- Yossef Gutfreund (wrestling referee, 40)

**Demands:** Release of 234 Palestinian and non-Palestinian prisoners held in Israeli prisons. List includes members of the German Red Army Faction (Baader group).

- **German response:** Bavarian authorities establish communications with terrorists. German police (GSG 9 not yet formed; standard police forces handle negotiation) coordinate response
- **Source:** [Britannica: Munich massacre](https://www.britannica.com/event/Munich-massacre)

### 10:00–10:30 – Transport to Helicopters

The terrorists move their bound and blindfolded hostages out of the dormitory into transport. Two helicopters (German Army Bundeswehr) are being prepared at the Olympic helipad. The Palestinians demand a plane at Munich-Riem Airport to transport hostages to the Middle East.

- **Location:** Olympic Village helicopter pad to German military helicopters
- **Source:** [Wikipedia: Munich massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich_massacre); [Britannica: Munich massacre](https://www.britannica.com/event/Munich-massacre)

### 10:30–22:00 – Helicopter Transfer

The two helicopters depart the Olympic Village with the hostages and their captors. They fly toward Fürstenfeldbruck air base (~20 km northwest of Munich), where German authorities have told the terrorists a Boeing 727 awaits them.

- **Actual situation:** No plane is present. The airbase is surrounded by German police sharpshooters who are to attempt a rescue
- **Source:** [History.com: More Israeli hostages killed in Munich](https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/september-6/more-israeli-hostages-killed-in-munich)

### 22:30 – Helicopters Land at Fürstenfeldbruck

The two German Huey helicopters land on the tarmac at the military airbase.

- **Location:** Fürstenfeldbruck Air Base runway
- **Terrain:** Open tarmac makes rescue operation extremely difficult
- **Visibility:** Darkness, limited lighting
- **Terrorist awareness:** Some sources indicate the terrorists realized a rescue was being attempted
- **Source:** [Wikipedia: Munich massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich_massacre)

### 23:00 (11 PM) – Gunfire Erupts

A firefight breaks out on the tarmac at Fürstenfeldbruck. German police sharpshooters positioned on the airbase begin firing at the terrorists.

- **Terrorist response:** The Palestinians immediately begin executing hostages
- **First helicopter:** Four hostages are gunned down inside the helicopter
- **Second helicopter:** A grenade is tossed into the second helicopter, killing all five remaining hostages
- **German casualties:** One German police officer, Anton Fliegerbauer (32), is fatally shot while providing fire support from a control tower position (bullet strikes the side of his head)
- **Source:** [History.com: More Israeli hostages killed in Munich](https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/september-6/more-israeli-hostages-killed-in-munich); [Wikipedia: Anton Fliegerbauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Fliegerbauer)

### 23:00–01:30 (1:30 AM next day) – Extended Firefight

Gunfire continues for approximately 2.5 hours as German police attempt to neutralize the terrorists while the hostages are already dead.

**Terrorists killed during firefight:**

1. **Luttif Afif** ("Issa," leader)
2. **Yusuf Nazzal** ("Tony," deputy)
3. **Khalid Jawad** ("Salah")
4. **Ahmed Chic Thaa** ("Abu Halla")
5. **Afif Ahmed Hamid** ("Paolo")

**Terrorists who survived by feigning death:**

1. **Jamal Al-Gashey** (shot through right wrist)
2. **Mohammed Safady** (flesh wound to leg)
3. **Adnan Al-Gashey** (uninjured)

- **Source:** [Wikipedia: Munich massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich_massacre); [Wikipedia: Luttif Afif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luttif_Afif)

## WEDNESDAY, 6 SEPTEMBER 1972

### 01:30 – Firefight Ends

The gunfire at Fürstenfeldbruck airbase ceases. All nine Israeli hostages are confirmed dead. Five Palestinian terrorists are dead. One German police officer is dead.

**Death toll from rescue attempt:**
- 9 Israeli hostages (murdered by Palestinians)
- 5 Palestinians (killed by German police)
- 1 German police officer (killed in crossfire)

Total: 15 dead

- **Source:** [Wikipedia: Munich massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich_massacre)

### Morning – Aftermath and Release of Surviving Terrorists

The three surviving terrorists escape. They eventually reach Libya where they are granted refuge. On 29 October 1972, they are released through the hijacking of Lufthansa Flight 615 from Damascus to Frankfurt by Arab militants, allowing them to travel to Libya safely.

- **Source:** [Wikipedia: Lufthansa Flight 615](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lufthansa_Flight_615); [Wikiwand: Lillehammer affair](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Lillehammer_affair)

### Afternoon – Memorial Service at Olympic Stadium

A memorial service is held for the victims at the Munich Olympic Stadium. IOC President Avery Brundage announces the controversial decision to continue the Games. His statement that "the games must go on" is widely criticized.

- **Brundage's controversial remark:** At the memorial service, Brundage also draws a parallel between the murder of 11 Israelis and the IOC's decision to bar Rhodesia (Zimbabwe) from competition. The remark causes shock and outrage in the stadium and worldwide. Brundage later apologizes.
- **Games suspension:** The Olympics are suspended for one day (the 6th) to allow for the memorial service and initial aftermath, then resume on 7 September 1972
- **Source:** [After 40 Years, It's Time to Commemorate Munich](https://www.adl.org/resources/news/after-40-years-its-time-commemorate-munich); [NPR: 50 years ago, Munich Olympics massacre changed security](https://www.npr.org/2022/09/04/1116641214/munich-olympics-massacre-hostage-terrorism-israel-germany)

### Evening / Next Days

The bodies of the 11 Israeli victims and the German police officer are removed. The five dead Palestinian terrorists' bodies are delivered to Libya, where they receive heroes' funerals and full military honors.

- **Israeli burial:** Victims' bodies are repatriated to Israel for funeral and burial
- **Source:** [Wikipedia: Munich massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich_massacre)
