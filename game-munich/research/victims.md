# The Eleven Israeli Victims and the German Police Officer

This file records the names, ages, sports, and roles of the 11 Israeli athletes and coaches killed in the Munich massacre on September 5-6, 1972, plus the German police officer killed during the rescue attempt.

## Israeli Victims (11)

All 11 were killed on September 5-6, 1972.

### Moshe Weinberg
- **Role:** Wrestling coach
- **Age at death:** 32
- **Born:** 19 September 1939, Haifa, British Mandate of Palestine
- **Died:** 5 September 1972
- **Killed:** Shot by terrorists during initial invasion of the Israeli team quarters, approximately 4:30 AM on September 5. His body was thrown into the street.
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Moshe Weinberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moshe_Weinberg); [Twelve Months—Twelve Names blog](https://blog.juedisches-museum-muenchen.de/munichmassacre72-moshe-weinberg/)

### Yossef Romano
- **Role:** Weightlifter
- **Age at death:** 32
- **Born:** 15 April 1940, Benghazi, Italian Libya
- **Died:** 5 September 1972
- **Killed:** Shot by terrorists in the initial assault. He was a veteran of the 1967 Six-Day War.
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Yossef Romano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yossef_Romano); [Israel Education & Experiences: Yossef Romano Born Libya](https://israeled.org/israeli-olympian-yossef-romano-born-libya/)

### David Mark Berger
- **Role:** Weightlifter
- **Age at death:** 28
- **Born:** 24 May 1944, Cleveland, Ohio, USA
- **Died:** 6 September 1972
- **Citizenship:** Dual US-Israeli; the only American-born victim among the 11
- **Education:** Lawyer, attended Tulane University, New Orleans (1962–1966)
- **Killed:** Shot in helicopter during rescue attempt at Fürstenfeldbruck airbase
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: David Berger (weightlifter)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Berger_(weightlifter)); [Find a Grave Memorial](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/27568321/david-mark-berger)

### Ze'ev Friedman
- **Role:** Weightlifter (flyweight)
- **Age at death:** 28
- **Born:** 10 June 1944, Prokopyevsk, Soviet Union
- **Died:** 6 September 1972
- **Athletic achievement:** Bronze medal at 1971 Asian Weightlifting Championships; placed 12th at 1972 Olympics
- **Killed:** Shot in helicopter during rescue attempt at Fürstenfeldbruck
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Ze'ev Friedman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ze%27ev_Friedman); [Find a Grave Memorial](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/27568601/ze_ev-friedman)

### Eliezer Halfin
- **Role:** Wrestler (lightweight)
- **Age at death:** 24
- **Born:** 18 June 1948, Riga, Latvian SSR, Soviet Union
- **Died:** 6 September 1972
- **Immigration:** Came to Israel in 1969; became Israeli citizen seven months before his death
- **Killed:** Shot in helicopter during rescue attempt at Fürstenfeldbruck
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Eliezer Halfin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eliezer_Halfin); [Find a Grave Memorial](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/8663064/eliezer-halfin)

### Mark Slavin
- **Role:** Wrestler (Greco-Roman middleweight)
- **Age at death:** 18 (youngest victim)
- **Born:** 31 January 1954, Minsk, Belarus SSR, Soviet Union
- **Died:** 6 September 1972
- **Background:** Took up wrestling to defend himself against anti-Semitic attacks; won Soviet Greco-Roman wrestling middleweight junior championship in 1971
- **Immigration:** Moved to Israel just four months before the Olympics; joined Hapoel Tel Aviv
- **Olympic potential:** Considered Israel's most likely medal winner at Munich
- **Killed:** Shot by machine gun fire in helicopter during rescue attempt
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Mark Slavin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Slavin); [Find a Grave Memorial](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/8663009/mark-slavin)

### Amitzur Shapira
- **Role:** Track and field coach (head coach of Israeli team)
- **Age at death:** 40
- **Born:** 9 July 1932, Tel Aviv, Mandatory Palestine
- **Died:** 6 September 1972
- **Background:** Former sprinter and long jumper; served as teacher and educator at Wingate Institute
- **Killed:** Grenaded/shot in helicopter during rescue attempt at Fürstenfeldbruck
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Amitzur Shapira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amitzur_Shapira); [Find a Grave Memorial](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/8663021/amitzur-shapira)

### Kehat Shorr
- **Role:** Shooting coach (marksmanship coach)
- **Age at death:** 53
- **Born:** 21 February 1919, Romania
- **Died:** 6 September 1972
- **Background:** Expert marksman; moved to Israel in 1963; trained the national shooting team for Munich Olympics
- **Killed:** Shot in helicopter during rescue attempt at Fürstenfeldbruck
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Kehat Shorr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kehat_Shorr); [Munich Massacre 72 blog](https://blog.juedisches-museum-muenchen.de/munichmassacre72-kehat-schor/)

### Andre Spitzer
- **Role:** Fencing master and coach of fencing team
- **Age at death:** 27
- **Born:** 4 July 1945, Timișoara, Romania
- **Died:** 6 September 1972
- **Background:** Spoke fluent German; father died in 1956; mother and Andre moved to Israel; by age 27, known as Israel's top fencing coach
- **Incident:** During hostage crisis, seen tied up at window; when he tried to give negotiators information, a terrorist clubbed him in the head with an AK-47
- **Killed:** Shot in helicopter during rescue attempt
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Andre Spitzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andre_Spitzer); [Find a Grave Memorial](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/8663083/andre-spitzer)

### Yakov Springer
- **Role:** Weightlifting judge and coach
- **Age at death:** 51
- **Born:** 10 June 1921, Poland
- **Died:** 6 September 1972
- **Background:** Superb reputation as international judge; attending Munich for the fifth time as official judge at Olympic Games; survived Warsaw Ghetto Uprising during Holocaust
- **Killed:** Shot in helicopter during rescue attempt at Fürstenfeldbruck
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Yakov Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yakov_Springer); [Israel Olympics: Yakov Springer](https://www.olympicsil.co.il/yakov-springer/?lang=en)

### Yossef Gutfreund
- **Role:** Wrestling referee (judge)
- **Age at death:** 40
- **Born:** 1 November 1931, Craiova, Romania
- **Died:** 6 September 1972
- **Physical description:** 6 foot 3 inches tall, 290 pounds
- **Background:** This was his third Olympics as wrestling referee
- **Heroic action:** Heard noise at door around 4:30 AM, investigated, saw masked gunmen; threw his large frame against the door and screamed warning to roommates, giving fellow athlete Tuvia Sokolsky precious seconds to escape through a smashed window
- **Killed:** Shot/grenaded in helicopter during rescue attempt at Fürstenfeldbruck
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Yossef Gutfreund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yossef_Gutfreund); [Munich Massacre 72 blog](https://blog.juedisches-museum-muenchen.de/munichmassacre72-yossef-gutfreund/)

## German Police Officer Killed

### Anton Fliegerbauer
- **Title:** Police sergeant, Bavarian State Police (Bereitschaftspolizei)
- **Age at death:** 32
- **Born:** 5 March 1940, Westerndorf, Lower Bavaria, West Germany
- **Died:** 5-6 September 1972 (night of attack)
- **Personal:** Married to Maria in 1966; son Alfred born 1968
- **Training:** Bavarian State Police
- **Role:** Assigned to riot police (Bereitschaftspolizei) unit during Olympics
- **Death:** Killed during rescue attempt at Fürstenfeldbruck air base. Shot in the head (bullet struck the side of his head) while providing fire support from control tower position.
- **Burial:** Laid to rest 8 September 1972, Munich Waldfriedhof cemetery
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Anton Fliegerbauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Fliegerbauer); [Munich Massacre 72 blog](https://blog.juedisches-museum-muenchen.de/munichmassacre72-anton-fliegerbauer/)

## Memorials and Remembrance

### Erinnerungsort Olympia-Attentat (Memory Place of the Olympic Attack)
- **Location:** Munich Olympiapark
- **Opened:** 6 September 2017
- **Dedication:** Attended by Israeli President Reuven Rivlin and German President Frank-Walter Steinmeier
- **Design:** Large exhibition area carved into grass mound with effect of open wound
- **Features:** 11-meter-width video installation loop (10 minutes duration) shown daily 8 AM to 10 PM in German with English subtitles; presents victims' individual biographies with contemporary film sequences
- **Content:** Dedicated to 11 Israeli athletes and 1 German policeman
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Erinnerungsort Olympia-Attentat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erinnerungsort_Olympia-Attentat); [Jüdisches Museum München](https://www.juedisches-museum-muenchen.de/en/exhibitions/memorialtothe1972munichmassacre)

### Additional Munich Memorials
- **Stone tablet** at bridge linking Olympic stadium to former Olympic village, honoring the slain Israelis
- **Memorial tablet** at 31 Connollystraße (former Israeli team lodging)
- **Sources:** [fotoeins.com: Memorials](https://fotoeins.com/2018/09/04/muenchen-memorials-olympics-massacre/)

### Twelve Months—Twelve Names Project (50 Years Commemoration)
- **Organizer:** Jüdisches Museum München (Jewish Museum Munich)
- **Format:** Individual biographical and historical video presentations for each victim plus Fliegerbauer
- **Medium:** Blog series and exhibition
- **Sources:** [Jüdisches Museum München](https://www.juedisches-museum-muenchen.de/en/exhibitions/twelve-months-twelve-names); [nsdoku.de](https://www.nsdoku.de/en/program/current/twelve-months-twelve-names)
