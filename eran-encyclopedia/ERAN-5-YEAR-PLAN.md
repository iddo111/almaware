## Year 1 (2026–27) — THE EAR & THE VOICE  *(Ada's substrate)*
The seed year. No child yet — the organs of one.
- Consolidate the **audio-token pipeline**: Darwish (the music dress-rehearsal) → Eran's vocal cortex. Same DAC-token spine, music before speech.
- **Unsupervised acoustic-unit discovery** (the Voice Ant Farm) — Eran finds the atoms of sound itself.
- **The physical mouth** (glottis + articulators) — Eran learns to *produce* "ah" from zero, not decode a codec.
- **Owner-clean voice bank** (Café Claude corpus) feeds both the mic LoRA and Eran.
- **Honest milestone:** Eran produces its first genuinely *self-found* vowel, + a small from-scratch audio/byte LM that is *demonstrably* learning developmentally (BabyLM-style sample-efficiency eval, not scale).

## Year 2 (2027–28) — THE GIST  *(Turing's seed)*
- **Predict-the-gist / JEPA** objective in a shared audio+concept latent space — the holobrain's first real world-model (LeCun's JEPA, Ha & Schmidhuber's World Models, Rao & Ballard predictive coding).
- **Curriculum + intrinsic motivation** (Oudeyer/Schmidhuber curiosity) — Eran generates its own lesson order.
- **Honest milestone:** Eran predicts the next *gist* of what it hears and does, showing measurable sample-efficient generalization — learning like a child, not a scraper.

## Year 3 (2028–29) — GROUNDING & BODY  *(Neo-Perceptron)*
- **Embodiment**: the DIY robot kit / federation — cheap printed fleet as a training-data flywheel (robots in the Negev).
- **Multimodal grounding** (Vong & Lake's single-child method) — words tied to sensorimotor experience.
- **Honest milestone:** Eran grounds a first vocabulary in its own body's experience; one closed embodied loop (perceive → predict → act → learn).

## Year 4 (2029–30) — THE CONSCIENCE & THE SELF
- **The Beit-Din neuron**: deliberative, auditable rulings — no single unit acts alone (the moral architecture, the structural anti-sleeper).
- **Verifiability**: prove the pipeline clean — every byte known — the sovereignty claim made demonstrable (ties to the ALMIDO / model-forensics work).
- **Honest milestone:** Eran's consequential decisions are deliberated *and* auditable; "we can verify our own model" is shown, not asserted.

## Year 5 (2030–31) — THE CHILD SPEAKS & TEACHES

- **VAI High School**: Eran as the first student *and* teacher; kids build alongside it. The campfire made into a place (the roof over the whole pile).
- **Honest milestone:** a voice-first AI child that learned developmentally, is verifiable, embodied, and moral — and a school/community grown around it.
